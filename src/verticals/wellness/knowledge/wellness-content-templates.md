# Wellness Content Templates

Domain-specific content templates for wellness, health, mindfulness, and self-improvement content. Use these templates to structure content for each wellness topic.

---

## Meditation & Mindfulness

### Guided Meditation Post
- **Hook:** Start with a relatable stress scenario or emotional state
- **Body:** Introduce a simple meditation technique (3-5 steps)
- **CTA:** Invite audience to try it and share their experience
- **Format:** Carousel (step-by-step), Reel (guided walkthrough), or text post

### Mindfulness Tip
- **Hook:** "The next time you feel overwhelmed, try this..."
- **Body:** One actionable mindfulness technique with specific instructions
- **CTA:** Save for later, share with someone who needs it
- **Visual:** Calming imagery — nature, soft gradients, breathing animations

### Breathing Exercise
- **Hook:** "This 4-7-8 breathing technique can calm your nervous system in under 2 minutes"
- **Body:** Step-by-step breathing instructions with timing
- **CTA:** "Try it right now — comment how you feel after"
- **Format:** Reel with timer overlay, or carousel with breath count visuals

## Daily Affirmations

### Morning Affirmation
- **Hook:** "Start your day with this intention..."
- **Body:** Single powerful affirmation with context on why it works
- **CTA:** "What's your morning affirmation? Share below."
- **Visual:** Warm sunrise tones, clean typography, minimalist design

### Weekly Affirmation Series
- **Hook:** "This week's theme: [Self-Compassion / Resilience / Growth]"
- **Body:** 7 affirmations, one for each day
- **CTA:** "Save this carousel and revisit each morning"
- **Format:** Carousel (7 slides + cover), or daily Story series

### Affirmation Challenge
- **Hook:** "30-day affirmation challenge — are you in?"
- **Body:** Challenge rules, daily prompt structure, community hashtag
- **CTA:** "Share your Day 1 affirmation with #[BrandAffirmationChallenge]"
- **Format:** Carousel (overview), daily follow-up posts

## Workout & Fitness Motivation

### Quick Workout Post
- **Hook:** "No gym? No problem. 15 minutes, zero equipment."
- **Body:** 4-6 exercise descriptions with reps/sets
- **CTA:** "Tag your workout buddy"
- **Format:** Carousel (exercise demos), Reel (follow-along), text post

### Progress Celebration
- **Hook:** "Your progress isn't always visible — but it's real"
- **Body:** Motivational context about non-scale victories, consistency wins
- **CTA:** "Share a non-scale victory in the comments"
- **Visual:** Before/after journey (non-body-focused), progress chart graphics

### Recovery & Rest Day
- **Hook:** "Rest days aren't lazy days — they're growth days"
- **Body:** Science-backed benefits of recovery, active rest suggestions
- **CTA:** "What does your rest day look like?"
- **Format:** Carousel (rest day tips), text post

## Nutrition & Healthy Eating

### Healthy Recipe Share
- **Hook:** "This 5-ingredient meal takes 20 minutes and supports [gut health / energy / sleep]"
- **Body:** Ingredients list, simple instructions, nutritional context (not medical claims)
- **CTA:** "Save this recipe for meal prep day"
- **Format:** Carousel (step-by-step photos), Reel (cooking timelapse)

### Mindful Eating Tip
- **Hook:** "Eating slower isn't just good manners — it may support better digestion"
- **Body:** Practical mindful eating strategies
- **CTA:** "Try eating one meal today without your phone"
- **Visual:** Beautifully plated food, calm dining setting

### Hydration Reminder
- **Hook:** "If you haven't had water in the last 2 hours, this is your sign"
- **Body:** Creative hydration tips, water intake myths vs facts
- **CTA:** "Go drink some water and come back to like this post"
- **Format:** Simple text post or Story with interactive poll

## Mental Health Awareness

### Mental Health Check-In
- **Hook:** "How are you really doing today?"
- **Body:** Normalize talking about mental health, provide self-check questions
- **CTA:** "Drop a [emoji] that represents your mood today"
- **Visual:** Warm, inviting colors — avoid clinical imagery

### Coping Strategy Spotlight
- **Hook:** "When anxiety shows up, I reach for this one tool..."
- **Body:** One evidence-based coping strategy with step-by-step instructions
- **CTA:** "What's your go-to coping strategy? Share below."
- **Format:** Carousel (strategy breakdown), text post

### Stigma-Breaking Story
- **Hook:** "We need to normalize [asking for help / taking mental health days / therapy]"
- **Body:** Perspective shift, community encouragement, resources
- **CTA:** "Share this with someone who might need to hear it"
- **Important:** Never provide diagnostic advice. Always include crisis resources.

## Self-Care Routines

### Morning Routine
- **Hook:** "A morning routine that actually sets you up for a good day..."
- **Body:** 5-7 morning habits with timing suggestions
- **CTA:** "What's one thing you never skip in your morning?"
- **Format:** Carousel (habit-by-habit), Reel (routine montage)

### Evening Wind-Down
- **Hook:** "Your evening routine starts at 7pm — here's why"
- **Body:** Sleep hygiene tips, digital sunset, relaxation techniques
- **CTA:** "Save this for tonight"
- **Visual:** Warm lighting, cozy atmosphere, calming tones

### Self-Care Sunday
- **Hook:** "Your permission slip to do absolutely nothing today"
- **Body:** Self-care menu (physical, emotional, social, spiritual categories)
- **CTA:** "Pick one thing from each category — what's your Sunday plan?"
- **Format:** Carousel (category breakdown), interactive Story poll

## Stress Management

### Stress Relief Technique
- **Hook:** "Feeling the pressure? Try this 2-minute reset..."
- **Body:** Quick stress relief technique (grounding, progressive muscle relaxation, etc.)
- **CTA:** "Bookmark this for those overwhelming moments"
- **Format:** Reel (guided technique), carousel (step-by-step)

### Burnout Prevention
- **Hook:** "Burnout doesn't happen overnight — here are the early signs"
- **Body:** Warning signs checklist, prevention strategies
- **CTA:** "If you checked 3 or more, it might be time to reassess"
- **Format:** Carousel (checklist), text post with list
