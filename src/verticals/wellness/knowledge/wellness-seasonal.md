# Wellness Seasonal Calendar

Strategic content calendar aligned with wellness awareness events, seasons, and cultural moments. Plan content 2-4 weeks ahead of each event for optimal reach.

---

## January — New Year, New Habits

### Key Themes
- New Year wellness resolutions and goal-setting
- Habit formation science (21-day myth vs. 66-day average)
- "Fresh start" energy without toxic positivity
- Cold-weather wellness tips

### Key Dates
- **Jan 1 — New Year's Day:** Intention-setting posts (not just "resolutions")
- **Jan 11 — National Human Trafficking Awareness Day:** Mental health awareness angle
- **Jan 20 — Blue Monday (3rd Monday):** Combat the "most depressing day" narrative with positivity
- **Jan (full month) — National Mentoring Month:** Wellness mentorship, accountability partners

### Content Strategy
- High engagement period — wellness intent is peak
- Focus on achievable micro-goals over grand resolutions
- Carousel: "5 wellness habits that stick (backed by science)"

## May — Mental Health Awareness

### Key Themes
- **Mental Health Awareness Month** — biggest wellness content opportunity of the year
- Maternal mental health (Mother's Day connection)
- Physical activity for mental health
- End-of-school stress (parents & students)

### Key Dates
- **May 1-31 — Mental Health Awareness Month:** Daily content opportunity
- **May (2nd Sunday) — Mother's Day:** Maternal wellness, self-care for moms
- **May (1st full week) — Children's Mental Health Awareness Week**
- **May 10 — World Lupus Day:** Chronic illness and wellness

### Content Strategy
- **Highest-impact wellness month** — plan 20+ pieces of content
- Daily mental health tips carousel series
- Partner with mental health advocates for reach
- Always include crisis resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741)

- Back-to-school adjustment

### Key Dates
- **Sep 1-30 — National Suicide Prevention Awareness Month:** Life-saving content

- **Sep 10 — World Suicide Prevention Day:** Share crisis resources

### Content Strategy
