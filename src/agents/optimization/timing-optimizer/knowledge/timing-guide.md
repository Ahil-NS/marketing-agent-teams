# Timing Optimization Guide

## Platform Peak Times (US Eastern, general guidelines)
- **Reddit**: 6-9am EST weekdays, especially Tuesday-Thursday
- **TikTok**: 7-9am, 12-3pm, 7-11pm (varies by audience)
- **Instagram**: 11am-1pm, 7-9pm weekdays; 10am-12pm weekends
- **Facebook**: 1-4pm weekdays, especially Wednesday; 12-1pm weekends

## Timing Factors
- Audience timezone distribution (primary and secondary)
- Competition timing (when competitors post)
- Content type timing (educational AM, entertaining PM)
- Platform algorithm refresh cycles
- Breaking news and cultural moments

## Frequency Guidelines
- **Reddit**: 1-3 posts per week per subreddit (avoid spam)
- **TikTok**: 1-3 posts per day for growth
- **Instagram**: 3-5 feed posts per week, daily stories
- **Facebook**: 1-2 posts per day for pages

## Anti-Patterns
- Don't post at algorithm-determined "best times" if everyone does
- Don't exceed platform-specific frequency guidelines
- Don't post right before major events that will dominate feeds
- Don't ignore timezone differences for global audiences
