# Humanization Techniques

Anti-detection rewriting techniques for transforming AI-generated text into authentic human writing.

## Sentence Length Variation

Mix dramatically different sentence lengths within the same paragraph:
- Short punches: 3-8 words ("Here's the thing." / "Game changer." / "Not even close.")
- Medium flow: 12-18 words
- Long compounds: 25-40 words with subordinate clauses, dashes, or parentheticals

**Target:** Standard deviation of sentence length > 8 words per paragraph.

Example:
- AI: "Meditation can significantly improve your daily productivity. It helps you maintain focus throughout the day. Many professionals have found it beneficial for their work-life balance."
- Human: "Meditation changed everything for me. Like, I went from barely making it through my afternoon slump to actually crushing my to-do list — and I'm not even exaggerating. Try it."

## Deliberate Imperfections

Insert natural human writing artifacts:

### Contractions
- "It is" → "It's"
- "Do not" → "Don't"
- "They are" → "They're"
- "Would not" → "Wouldn't"

### Sentence Fragments
- "Absolutely worth it."
- "Not gonna lie."
- "Big difference."
- "The best part?"

### Rhetorical Questions
- "But does it actually work?"
- "Sound familiar?"
- "Know what I mean?"
- "Ever tried meditating at 5am? Yeah, me neither."

### Opinion Markers
- "Honestly, I think..."
- "Hot take:"
- "Unpopular opinion but..."
- "Look, here's what nobody tells you..."

### Self-Corrections and Asides
- "Well, sort of."
- "— okay, hear me out —"
- "(and yes, I was skeptical too)"

Adapt vocabulary and formatting to match how real users write on each platform. See platform-writing-styles.md for detailed per-platform guidance.

## Asymmetric Paragraph Lengths

Break the AI pattern of uniform paragraph sizes:
- Mix 1-sentence paragraphs with 5-6 sentence paragraphs
- Use a single-line paragraph for emphasis
- Vary positions of key information (not always in topic sentences)

Example pattern: 3 sentences → 1 sentence → 6 sentences → 2 sentences

## Opinion Insertion

Where appropriate and factually safe, insert subjective markers:
- "This is hands down the best approach I've tried"
- "I was honestly surprised by how well this worked"
- "Not for everyone, but if you're into [topic]..."

**Critical constraint:** Only insert opinions that don't alter factual claims.

## Personal Anecdotes Style

Frame information as if sharing personal experience:
- "I've been doing this for about 3 months now..."
- "A friend recommended this to me last year..."
- "After trying literally everything else..."

**Critical constraint:** Don't invent specific claims. Use the style/framing only.

## Conversational Connectors

Replace formal transitions with natural conversational bridges:
- "Furthermore" → "Plus" / "And honestly" / "Oh, and"
- "However" → "But here's the thing" / "That said"
- "In conclusion" → (just end naturally, no conclusion marker)
- "Additionally" → "Also" / "Another thing—"
- "It is worth mentioning" → (just state it directly)

## Structure Variation Techniques

### Start Mid-Thought
Instead of a proper introduction, start as if continuing a conversation:
- "So I've been testing this wellness app for the past month..."
- "Okay but can we talk about how underrated meditation is?"

### End Without Wrapping Up
Skip the conclusion paragraph entirely. Human posts often just... stop.

### Break the Pattern
- Put the most important info in the middle, not the beginning
- Start with a question
- Use a one-word paragraph for dramatic effect
- Insert a tangent that circles back
