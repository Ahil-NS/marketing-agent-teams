# Hashtag Research Methodology

## How to Evaluate Hashtag Reach and Competition

### Reach Estimation
- **High reach:** Hashtag has >1M posts/uses across the platform; broad audience exposure
- **Medium reach:** 100K-1M posts; balanced discoverability without excessive competition
- **Low reach:** <100K posts; niche audience but higher engagement rate per impression

### Competition Assessment
- **High competition:** Top posts dominated by major brands/influencers; difficult to rank
- **Medium competition:** Mix of established and emerging creators; achievable visibility
- **Low competition:** Underserved space; good opportunity for early adoption

### Relevance Scoring (0-100)
- 90-100: Directly describes the content topic or brand
- 70-89: Closely related to content theme or audience interest
- 50-69: Tangentially related; may attract adjacent audiences
- Below 50: Do not recommend — too loosely connected

## Seasonal and Trending Patterns

### Identifying Trends
- Use WebSearch to check current platform trending topics
- Look for event-driven hashtags (holidays, awareness months, industry events)
- Monitor competitor hashtag usage for emerging patterns
- Check platform-specific trending sections (TikTok Discover, Instagram Explore)

### Seasonal Considerations
- Plan for upcoming seasonal events (2-4 weeks ahead)
- Rotate seasonal hashtags as relevance changes
- Evergreen hashtags provide consistent baseline performance
- Combine seasonal + evergreen for optimal results

## Branded vs Community Hashtag Strategy

### Branded Hashtags
- Include brand name or campaign name
- Use for cross-platform campaign tracking
- Keep short, memorable, and unique
- Verify no existing negative associations
- Example: #BrandNameWellness, #CampaignName2026

### Community Hashtags
- Originated by or widely used within a specific community
- Signal insider knowledge and authenticity
- Higher engagement from community members
- Must be used authentically — misuse damages credibility

## Hashtag Hygiene

### Avoid These
- **Banned hashtags:** Platforms periodically ban certain hashtags; check before recommending
- **Shadow-banned hashtags:** Content using these won't appear in hashtag search results
- **Controversial hashtags:** May attract negative attention or associate brand with unintended topics
- **Overused generic hashtags:** (#love, #happy, #instagood) provide no targeting value
- **Irrelevant trending hashtags:** Using unrelated trends is seen as spam

### Quality Checks
1. Search the hashtag and review what content appears
2. Verify the hashtag isn't banned or restricted on the target platform
3. Confirm the hashtag audience aligns with the content's target audience
4. Check for any negative or controversial associations
5. Ensure the hashtag hasn't been hijacked for unrelated content
