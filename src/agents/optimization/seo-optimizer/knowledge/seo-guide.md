# SEO Best Practices (2026)

## Keyword Research
- Focus on user intent: informational, navigational, transactional
- Target mix of head terms and long-tail keywords
- Analyze SERP features for each keyword
- Consider keyword difficulty vs. domain authority
- Use platform-native search tools to validate trending keywords

## On-Page Optimization
- Primary keyword in title, H1, first paragraph
- Secondary keywords in H2s and body content
- Optimal keyword density: 1-3% depending on platform (natural inclusion)
- Alt text for all images (mandatory for Instagram)
- Internal links to related content where platform supports them

## Platform-Specific SEO (2026 Current)

### TikTok SEO — 4 Indexable Layers
TikTok indexes content across 4 distinct layers for search and recommendations:
1. **Caption Text** (max 4000 chars): Front-load keywords in the first line for maximum indexing weight. The caption is the primary text signal.
2. **OCR Text Overlay**: On-screen text is scanned and indexed. Include primary keywords in text overlays for double-indexing.
3. **Audio Keywords**: TikTok transcribes spoken audio. Include primary keyword in spoken audio within the first 5 seconds.
4. **Hashtags**: Use 3-5 niche hashtags. Avoid generic tags (#fyp, #foryou, #foryoupage, #viral) as they dilute discoverability.

- Ranking signals: watch-time, shares, comments, profile visits, saves
- Trending sound integration increases distribution

### Reddit SEO — Google Search Visibility
Reddit posts are heavily indexed by Google (2026). Optimize for both Reddit internal search and Google:
- **Title**: Max 300 chars, optimal 60 chars. Front-load keywords for both Reddit search and Google SERP.
- **Body**: Max 40K chars, optimal 300-500 words. Keyword-rich first paragraph for Google snippet extraction.
- **Zero hashtags**: Reddit does not use hashtags. Never include them.
- **Subreddit tone matching**: Content must match the tone and norms of the target subreddit.
- Ranking signals: upvotes, comment count, awards, crosspost count

### Facebook SEO — Comment-Weight Algorithm
Facebook's 2026 algorithm heavily weights comment engagement:
- **Post length**: Max 63K chars, optimal 40-80 chars for engagement.
- **Hashtags**: 1-2 maximum. More than 2 reduces reach.
- **Comment weight optimization**: Posts that generate comments rank higher. Use questions and prompts.
- **Video preference**: Algorithm favors video content. Include video preference signals.
- Ranking signals: comments, shares, reactions, video watch-time

### Instagram SEO — Saves+Shares Ranking
Instagram's 2026 algorithm ranks saves and shares as the top engagement signals:
- **Caption**: Max 2200 chars, but only first 125 chars visible before "more" truncation. Place primary keywords within the first 125 visible characters.
- **Alt text**: Required, 100-125 chars optimal. Include descriptive keywords for accessibility and search.
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 hashtags (official Instagram recommendation for 2026). Place in caption body, not comments.
- **Saves+shares weighting**: Content optimized for saves and shares ranks significantly higher than likes-only content.
- Ranking signals: saves, shares, comments, profile visits, follows

## Content Structure
- Use clear heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bullet points and numbered lists for scanability
- Table of contents for long-form content
- Platform-specific formatting (Reddit markdown, TikTok plain text)

## Cross-Platform Optimization Rules
- Never apply the same SEO strategy across all platforms
- Each platform has unique character limits, hashtag rules, and ranking signals
- Keyword density targets vary: TikTok 1-3%, Reddit 0.5-2%, Facebook 0.5-2%, Instagram 1-3%
- Always validate content against platform-specific character limits before submission
