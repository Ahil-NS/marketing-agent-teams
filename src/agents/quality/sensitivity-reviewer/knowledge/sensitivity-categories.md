# Sensitivity Categories

## Cultural Sensitivity
- **Stereotypes**: Generalizations about cultural groups ("Asians are good at math", "French people are romantic")
- **Cultural appropriation**: Using sacred or significant cultural elements out of context (tribal patterns, religious symbols as decoration)
- **Ethnocentrism**: Presenting one culture's norms as universal ("everyone celebrates Christmas")
- **Food/diet assumptions**: Assuming all audiences eat certain foods (pork, beef, alcohol)
- **Name/language assumptions**: Mocking accents, using foreign words incorrectly, assuming English fluency

## Political Sensitivity
- **Partisan language**: Words/phrases associated with specific political movements
- **Controversial policies**: References to immigration, gun control, abortion, etc.
- **Geopolitical issues**: Territory disputes, sanctions, national conflicts
- **Government criticism**: Statements that could be problematic in certain markets
- **Electoral references**: Endorsing or appearing to endorse political candidates

## Religious Sensitivity
- **Sacred imagery**: Using religious symbols decoratively (cross, crescent, Om)
- **Dietary references**: Assuming non-halal, non-kosher diets
- **Holiday assumptions**: "Merry Christmas" to diverse audiences, assuming everyone observes same holidays
- **Religious practices**: Trivializing prayer, meditation from spiritual traditions, sacred rituals
- **Interfaith tensions**: Content that might be seen as favoring one religion

## Gender & Identity
- **Gendered language**: "businessman" → "businessperson", "mankind" → "humankind"
- **Role assumptions**: "moms will love this" (excludes dads), "his secretary" (gender assumption)
- **Binary assumptions**: "men and women" without acknowledging non-binary people
- **Heteronormative language**: "husband and wife" when "partner" is more inclusive
- **Body image**: Comments about weight, appearance, beauty standards

## Racial Sensitivity
- **Stereotypes**: Any racial generalization, even "positive" ones
- **Color metaphors**: "blacklist/whitelist" → "blocklist/allowlist", "dark day" for bad things
- **Historical insensitivity**: Trivializing slavery, colonialism, genocide
- **Tokenism**: Using diversity as a marketing prop without substance
- **Exclusionary language**: "normal" to describe the majority culture/race

## Ableist Language
- **Disability metaphors**: "blind to the truth", "deaf to criticism", "lame excuse"
- **Ability assumptions**: "walk into any store", "see for yourself" (can exclude disabled persons)
- **Mental health language**: "crazy deal", "insane prices", "OCD about details"
- **Intelligence language**: "idiot-proof", "no-brainer", "dumb question"
- **Physical assumptions**: Assuming all users can type, click, stand, etc.

## Ageist Language
- **Generational stereotypes**: "OK boomer", "lazy millennials", "Gen Z attention spans"
- **Age-based assumptions**: "even your grandma can use it", "not your parents' product"
- **Youth bias**: "young and dynamic team", implying youth equals energy/innovation
- **Aging language**: "despite their age", "still active", using age as a limitation

## Other Categories
- **Sexual content**: Inappropriate innuendo, objectification, suggestive imagery
- **Violence**: Aggressive metaphors ("crush the competition", "killer app"), war imagery
- **Profanity**: Explicit language, crude humor, double entendres
- **Controversial topics**: Divisive current events, conspiracy theories, medical controversies
