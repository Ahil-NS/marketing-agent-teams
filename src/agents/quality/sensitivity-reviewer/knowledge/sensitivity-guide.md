# Sensitivity Review Guide

## Review Dimensions
- **Cultural sensitivity**: Respect for different cultures and traditions
- **Language inclusivity**: Gender-neutral language, no ableist terms
- **Representation**: Diverse perspectives reflected in content
- **Stereotypes**: Avoid reinforcing harmful stereotypes
- **Context awareness**: Current events that change content sensitivity

## Common Sensitivity Issues
- Gendered language when gender-neutral alternatives exist
- Cultural appropriation vs. appreciation
- Assumptions about family structure, lifestyle, or values
- Economic privilege assumptions
- Ability-related language and assumptions
- Age-related stereotypes
- Religious or spiritual insensitivity

## Assessment Framework
- **Low risk**: Minor language adjustments recommended
- **Medium risk**: Content could offend specific groups — revisions recommended
- **High risk**: Content could cause significant harm — requires rewrite or removal

## Inclusive Language Guidelines
- Use "they/them" as default when gender is unknown
- Avoid idioms that may not translate across cultures
- Use people-first language ("people with disabilities")
- Avoid assumptions about audience demographics
- Consider accessibility for users with disabilities
