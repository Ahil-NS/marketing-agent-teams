# Severity Rating Rubric

## Critical
Content that could cause **legal issues, platform bans, or significant brand damage**.

### Examples
- Hate speech or slurs targeting any group
- Content promoting illegal discrimination
- Direct mockery of protected groups
- Content violating anti-discrimination laws
- Use of trademarked religious/cultural symbols in offensive ways
- Content that could be interpreted as inciting violence

### Action
**Block immediately** — content must not be published under any circumstances.

## High
Content **offensive to identifiable groups**, likely to generate complaints or backlash.

### Examples
- Racial or ethnic stereotypes, even "positive" ones
- Content trivializing historical atrocities or trauma
- Explicit mocking of religious practices or beliefs
- Content objectifying people based on gender/appearance
- Appropriation of sacred cultural elements for commercial use
- Content that reinforces harmful power dynamics

### Action
**Block** — content needs significant revision before publication.

## Medium
Content that **may be controversial in some contexts or regions**.

### Examples
- Gendered language that excludes non-binary individuals
- Assumptions about family structure (nuclear family default)
- Cultural references that don't translate across regions
- Ableist language used metaphorically ("blind spot", "lame")
- Age-based marketing assumptions
- Content that assumes economic privilege

### Action
**Needs revision** — suggest inclusive alternatives. Content can proceed if revised.

## Low
**Minor sensitivity issues** that some readers might notice.

### Examples
- Slightly informal language that may not suit all cultures
- Minor gendered pronoun usage that could be more neutral
- Idioms that don't translate well internationally
- Subtle assumptions about technology access or literacy
- References to alcohol/caffeine that may not suit all audiences

### Action
**Pass with warnings** — note for awareness. Author can decide whether to revise.

## Info
Noted for **awareness** but generally acceptable in marketing context.

### Examples
- Use of marketing competitiveness language ("beat the competition")
- Seasonal references that are region-specific
- Humor that could be misread in written form
- Cultural references that are widely understood
- Colloquial language appropriate for the target platform

### Action
**Pass** — no action required. Noted only for the author's awareness.
