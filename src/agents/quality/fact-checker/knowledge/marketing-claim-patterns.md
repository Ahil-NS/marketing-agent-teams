# Marketing Claim Patterns

## Common Claims Requiring Verification

### User/Customer Statistics
- "X% of users/customers report/prefer/agree..."
- "Used by X million people worldwide"
- "X users joined in the last month"
- **Verify**: Look for the source survey, sample size, and methodology

### Research References
- "Studies show that..."
- "Research indicates..."
- "According to experts..."
- "Scientifically proven..."
- **Verify**: Find the specific study. Generic "studies show" without citation is unverifiable

### Performance Claims
- "X% faster/better/more effective"
- "Reduces costs by X%"
- "Increases productivity by X%"
- **Verify**: Find benchmark data and methodology. "Faster than what?" matters

### Market Position Claims
- "The #1 platform for..."
- "Market leader in..."
- "Trusted by X% of Fortune 500"
- **Verify**: Check industry reports (Gartner, Forrester quadrants, market share data)

## Claims That Are Opinions, NOT Facts

### Marketing Superlatives — SKIP These
- "Best-in-class" — subjective, no universal standard
- "World-class" — no measurable definition
- "Revolutionary" — opinion about impact
- "Leading" — without market data, this is opinion
- "Premium" — subjective quality assessment
- "Cutting-edge" — opinion about innovation level
- "Unmatched" — vague comparative without benchmark

### Aspirational Language — SKIP These
- "Transform your business"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Take your game to the next level"

## Industry-Specific Claim Patterns

### Wellness / Health
- **HIGH RISK**: "Cures", "treats", "prevents disease" — FDA regulated claims
- **MEDIUM RISK**: "Supports immune health", "promotes relaxation" — structure-function claims
- **LOW RISK**: "May help with...", "Some users report..." — hedged language
- **SKIP**: "Feel better", "improve your wellness" — subjective/aspirational

### Technology / SaaS
- **VERIFY**: Uptime percentages ("99.9% uptime")
- **VERIFY**: Speed claims ("loads in under 2 seconds")
- **VERIFY**: Integration counts ("connects with 500+ tools")
- **SKIP**: "Intuitive interface", "seamless experience"

### Finance
- **HIGH RISK**: Return percentages, yield claims — SEC regulated
- **MEDIUM RISK**: Cost savings projections
- **VERIFY**: Historical performance data
- **SKIP**: "Smart investing", "financial freedom"
