# Platform-Specific Policies — Compliance Knowledge Base

## Reddit Content Policy

### Advertising & Promotional Content
- **Transparency**: Sponsored content must be clearly identified. Use `[Sponsored]` or `[Ad]` prefix in titles.
- **Self-promotion**: Reddit's informal "10% rule" — no more than 10% of submissions should be self-promotional.
- **Native advertising**: Disguising ads as organic posts violates Reddit's content policy.
- **Subreddit rules**: Individual subreddits may have stricter promotional content rules. Always check sidebar rules.

### Prohibited Content
- Spam and vote manipulation
- Misleading or deceptive content
- Content that incites violence or promotes hate
- Doxxing or sharing personal information
- Copyright-infringing content
- Sexual content involving minors

### Content Formatting Requirements
- Promotional posts should be clearly tagged
- Affiliate links should be disclosed in the post body
- Reddit does not support `#hashtag` formatting — disclosures must be in plain text
- Cross-posting promotional content across many subreddits is considered spam

## TikTok Community Guidelines

### Branded Content
- **Branded Content Toggle**: Creators MUST use the branded content toggle for any paid partnerships
- **`#ad` requirement**: Include `#ad` in caption for all sponsored content
- **Disclosure timing**: Verbal disclosure should occur within the first few seconds of the video
- **Duets and stitches**: Both original and response videos need separate disclosures if sponsored

### Prohibited Content Categories
- Misinformation related to health, elections, or public safety
- Dangerous challenges or activities
- Harassment and bullying
- Hate speech and discrimination
- Graphic or violent content
- Sexual content or nudity
- Minor safety violations
- Promoting illegal activities

### Health & Wellness Claims on TikTok
- No unverified health claims or medical advice
- Fitness content must not promote dangerous diets or extreme behaviors
- Mental health content must not replace professional care
- Weight loss claims require substantiation
- "Before and after" content may be restricted depending on context

### Music and Copyright
- Only use TikTok-licensed sounds for commercial/branded content
- Original audio is always safe
- User-generated sounds may have licensing restrictions
- Commercial music library available for business accounts

## Facebook/Meta Advertising Policies

### Prohibited Advertising Content
- **Tobacco products**: Includes e-cigarettes and vaping products
- **Drugs and drug-related products**: Recreational drugs, drug paraphernalia
- **Weapons and ammunition**: Firearms, explosives, modifications
- **Adult products**: In most ad placements; limited exceptions for age-gated targeting
- **Unsafe supplements**: Products deemed unsafe by regulatory authorities
- **Payday loans**: Short-term loans with high APR

### Restricted Advertising Content (Requires Special Handling)
- **Alcohol**: Age and country targeting required
- **Dating services**: Must comply with special dating ad policies
- **Real money gambling**: Requires prior authorization
- **Cryptocurrency**: May require special permissions
- **Social issues, elections, politics**: Requires "Paid for by" disclaimer and authorization

### Personal Attributes Policy
- Ads MUST NOT assert or imply personal attributes including:
  - Race, ethnicity, or national origin
  - Religion or philosophical belief
  - Age (except age-gated products)
  - Sexual orientation or gender identity
  - Disability or medical condition
  - Financial status or credit score
  - Criminal record
  - Name (e.g., "Hey John, try this!")

### Health Claims in Facebook Ads
- Before-and-after images: Heavily restricted; may be disapproved
- Weight loss claims: Must not imply guaranteed results
- Medical device claims: Must comply with local regulations
- Supplement ads: Cannot make disease claims

### Landing Page Requirements
- Landing page must match ad content and claims
- No deceptive redirect chains
- Product must be accurately represented
- Price and availability must be transparent

## Instagram Branded Content Rules

### Branded Content Requirements
- **Paid Partnership Tag**: Use Instagram's "Paid partnership with [brand name]" tag for ALL sponsored content
- **Branded Content Ads**: Sponsors can promote tagged branded content as ads — must comply with all Meta ad policies
- **FTC Compliance**: `#ad` or `#sponsored` must appear above the "more" fold in captions
- **Stories**: Each story frame with branded content must include the "Paid partnership" label

### Reels-Specific Rules
- Branded content toggle required for paid partnerships in Reels
- `#ad` must be in the caption (Reels captions are often truncated — disclosure must be visible)
- Audio: use Instagram's commercial music library for branded content
- Remix: both original and remix need disclosures if either is sponsored

### Shopping & Product Tags
- Product tags must link to the actual product being showcased
- Price must match the linked product page
- Cannot tag products that are out of stock or discontinued
- Affiliate shopping tags require disclosure

### Content Restrictions
- No promotion of tobacco, weapons, or adult products
- Health claims must comply with Meta advertising policies
- Financial services ads require disclaimers
- Contests/giveaways must include official rules and terms
- No artificial engagement tactics (follow/like loops, engagement pods)
