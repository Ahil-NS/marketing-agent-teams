# Platform Compliance Guide

## Common Policy Areas
- Prohibited content (hate speech, violence, illegal products)
- Advertising disclosure requirements
- Health and wellness claim restrictions
- Financial product advertising rules
- Political and social issue advertising
- Data collection and privacy notices

## Platform-Specific Notes

### Reddit
- Follow subreddit rules (unique per sub)
- Ads must be clearly labeled
- No vote manipulation or brigading
- Follow Reddit's Content Policy and Advertising Policy

### TikTok
- Community Guidelines restrict certain content types
- Ad disclosures required for paid partnerships
- Music licensing for commercial use
- Age-gated content requirements

### Instagram
- Branded content tool for paid partnerships
- FTC disclosure requirements
- Restricted content categories
- Community Guidelines on authenticity

### Facebook
- Advertising Standards for specific industries
- Special Ad Categories (housing, employment, credit)
- Political ad transparency requirements
- Community Standards enforcement

## Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Content meets all platform community guidelines
- [ ] Advertising disclosures properly included
- [ ] No prohibited content or claims
- [ ] Age targeting appropriate
- [ ] Industry-specific regulations met
