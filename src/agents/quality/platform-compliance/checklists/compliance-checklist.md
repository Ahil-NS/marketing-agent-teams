# Platform Compliance Checklist

## General Compliance
- [ ] No prohibited content (hate speech, violence, illegal)
- [ ] Advertising disclosures properly included
- [ ] No misleading or deceptive claims
- [ ] Content is age-appropriate for target audience
- [ ] Privacy and data collection notices where required

## Platform-Specific
- [ ] Meets platform advertising standards
- [ ] Follows community guidelines
- [ ] Proper use of sponsored/partner labels
- [ ] Content format meets platform specifications
- [ ] No banned words or phrases for the platform

## Industry-Specific
- [ ] Health claims are substantiated
- [ ] Financial disclaimers included where needed
- [ ] Environmental claims are verifiable
- [ ] Testimonials comply with FTC guidelines
