# Performance Analysis Guide

## Key Metrics by Platform

### Reddit
- Upvote ratio, comment count, cross-posts
- Post reach (views), engagement rate
- Subscriber impact, karma contribution

### TikTok
- Views, completion rate, shares, comments
- Follower growth, profile visits
- Sound and hashtag performance

### Instagram
- Reach, impressions, saves, shares
- Engagement rate, follower growth
- Story completion rate, Reel plays
- Hashtag reach contribution

### Facebook
- Reach, impressions, reactions, comments, shares
- Click-through rate, video views
- Group engagement, post boost performance

## Analysis Frameworks
- Compare to previous period (week-over-week, month-over-month)
- Benchmark against industry averages
- Attribution by content pillar
- Platform-specific performance trends
- Content format performance comparison

## Reporting Best Practices
- Lead with key takeaways
- Include visual data representations
- Show trends, not just snapshots
- Connect metrics to business objectives
- Provide clear next steps
