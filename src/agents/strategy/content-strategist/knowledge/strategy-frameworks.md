# Content Strategy Frameworks

## Content Pillar Model
- Each pillar represents a core theme that supports brand authority
- Pillars should be broad enough for ongoing content but specific enough for audience relevance
- Typical brand has 3-5 pillars covering different audience needs
- Each pillar should support multiple content formats

## Content Funnel Mapping
- **Awareness**: Educational, entertaining, trend-riding content
- **Consideration**: How-to guides, comparisons, case studies
- **Conversion**: Testimonials, product demos, special offers
- **Retention**: Community content, tips, exclusive insights

## Platform-Content Matrix
- Match content formats to platform strengths
- Adapt messaging depth per platform (short-form vs. long-form)
- Consider native features (polls, carousels, stories, reels)
- Plan cross-posting vs. platform-exclusive content

## Editorial Calendar Best Practices
- Maintain 70/20/10 content mix (proven/experimental/reactive)
- Build in flexibility for trend-jacking opportunities
- Batch similar content types for production efficiency
- Schedule reviews and optimization checkpoints
