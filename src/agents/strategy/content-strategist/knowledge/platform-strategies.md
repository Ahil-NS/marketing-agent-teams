# Platform-Specific Campaign Strategies

## Reddit Campaign Approaches

### Community-First Strategy
- Research target subreddits thoroughly before posting
- Build karma through genuine value contributions (comments, helpful answers)
- Content must provide standalone value — never pure promotion
- AMA (Ask Me Anything) format for brand introductions and thought leadership
- Share research findings, data, and insights the community would find useful

### Reddit Content Types That Work
- **Long-form text posts:** In-depth analysis, how-to guides, lesson-learned stories
- **Data-driven posts:** Charts, infographics, research findings with citations
- **Discussion starters:** Open-ended questions that invite community knowledge sharing
- **Link posts with context:** External content WITH commentary explaining why it's relevant
- **Series posts:** Multi-part content that builds audience over time

### Reddit Anti-Patterns
- Self-promotion without value (will be downvoted and potentially banned)
- Cross-posting promotional content across multiple subreddits
- Ignoring subreddit rules and culture
- Corporate tone or marketing-speak
- Not responding to comments on your posts

## TikTok Campaign Approaches

### Trend-Riding Strategy
- Monitor trending sounds, effects, and formats daily
- Adapt trends to your brand niche within 24-48 hours of emergence
- Create content that feels native to the platform, not repurposed ads
- Use trend-aware hooks: "POV:", "Wait for it...", "Things nobody tells you about..."

### TikTok Content Types That Work
- **Educational quick-tips:** 15-60 second lessons with text overlays
- **Behind-the-scenes:** Raw, authentic looks at your business/process
- **Transformation/before-after:** Visual proof of value
- **Stitch/Duet collaborations:** Engage with community content
- **Day-in-the-life:** Relatable, personality-driven content
- **Storytelling series:** Multi-part stories that drive follow-for-Part-2

### TikTok Authenticity Markers
- Vertical video only (9:16)
- Raw/unpolished often outperforms produced content
- First-person narration preferred
- Text overlays for silent viewing (85%+ watch without sound)
- 3-second hook is critical — front-load the most interesting element

## Facebook Campaign Approaches

### Community Groups Strategy
- Create or participate in relevant Facebook Groups
- Share valuable content that generates discussion threads
- Use Facebook Live for real-time engagement (Q&A, behind-the-scenes)
- Meaningful interactions (long comments, discussions) boost algorithm placement

### Facebook Content Types That Work
- **Long-form video:** 3-10 minute videos with strong narratives
- **Photo albums/stories:** Behind-the-scenes, process documentation
- **Discussion posts:** Questions that prompt genuine community responses
- **Live video:** Product launches, Q&A, tutorials, events
- **Groups content:** Exclusive tips, early access, community challenges

### Facebook Engagement Drivers
- Posts that generate meaningful comments (not just reactions) get algorithm boost
- Video content, especially native uploads, receives priority distribution
- Content that sparks conversation between commenters (comment threads) performs well
- Sharing within Groups amplifies organic reach significantly

## Instagram Campaign Approaches

### Visual-First Strategy
- Every content piece must be visually compelling first
- Consistent aesthetic grid that tells brand story at a glance
- Carousel posts for educational content (highest save rate)
- Reels for reach, Feed posts for engagement, Stories for relationship building

### Instagram Content Types That Work
- **Carousel posts:** Step-by-step guides, listicles, educational content (5-10 slides)
- **Reels:** Short-form video under 90 seconds, trending audio, quick tutorials
- **Stories:** Daily updates, polls, questions, behind-the-scenes, link stickers
- **Feed photos:** High-quality branded imagery, quotes, announcements
- **Collab posts:** Partnerships with relevant creators for cross-audience exposure

### Instagram Algorithm Priorities
- Reels receive the widest distribution (Explore page, Reels tab)
- Carousel saves signal high value to the algorithm
- Story engagement (polls, questions, sliders) boosts profile visibility
- Consistent posting cadence matters more than volume
- Early engagement (first 30 minutes) heavily influences distribution
