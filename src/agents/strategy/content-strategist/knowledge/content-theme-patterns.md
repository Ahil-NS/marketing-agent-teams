# Content Theme Templates

## Educational Themes

### 1. Expert Tips Series
- Quick, actionable advice from industry expertise
- Format: short-form video, carousel, text post
- Platform fit: All platforms (adapt format per platform)
- Engagement driver: Saves, shares, "tag someone who needs this"

### 2. Myth-Busting
- Challenge common misconceptions in your industry
- Format: before/after comparison, thread, video
- Platform fit: Reddit (analytical), TikTok (quick reveals), Instagram (carousels)
- Engagement driver: Comments, debates, shares

### 3. How-To Guides
- Step-by-step instructions for solving specific problems
- Format: carousel, long-form video, Reddit text post
- Platform fit: Instagram (carousel), TikTok (quick tutorial), Reddit (detailed guide)
- Engagement driver: Saves, bookmarks

### 4. Industry Insights
- Data-driven analysis of trends and patterns
- Format: infographics, charts, video breakdowns
- Platform fit: Reddit (data appreciation), Instagram (visual data), Facebook (discussion)
- Engagement driver: Shares, professional credibility

## Entertainment Themes

### 5. Behind-the-Scenes
- Raw, authentic look at your business/process
- Format: video, photo series, Stories
- Platform fit: TikTok (raw video), Instagram (Stories + Reels), Facebook (Live)
- Engagement driver: Authenticity connection, comments

### 6. Relatable Humor
- Industry-specific memes, situations, and observations
- Format: memes, short video, GIFs
- Platform fit: Reddit (meme culture), TikTok (trend adaptation), Instagram (Reels)
- Engagement driver: Shares, tags

### 7. Storytelling
- Narrative-driven content about your journey, customers, or mission
- Format: long-form video, carousel story, text post
- Platform fit: Facebook (storytelling culture), Instagram (carousel), Reddit (text)
- Engagement driver: Emotional connection, comments

### 8. Day-in-the-Life
- Follow a person through their routine, showcasing product/service naturally
- Format: video (vlog style)
- Platform fit: TikTok (primary), Instagram (Reels), YouTube Shorts
- Engagement driver: Relatability, curiosity

## Promotional Themes

### 9. Product Showcase
- Feature highlights, demos, and use cases
- Format: short video, carousel, live demo
- Platform fit: Instagram (visual product shots), TikTok (demo videos), Facebook (detailed posts)
- Engagement driver: Product interest, saves

### 10. Customer Success Stories
- Real customer results and testimonials
- Format: video testimonial, before/after, case study
- Platform fit: All platforms (adapt format)
- Engagement driver: Social proof, trust building

### 11. Limited-Time Offers
- Urgency-driven promotional content
- Format: Stories, Reels, announcements
- Platform fit: Instagram (Stories), Facebook (Events), TikTok (LIVE)
- Engagement driver: FOMO, urgency

### 12. Product Comparison
- Side-by-side comparisons with alternatives
- Format: carousel, video, chart
- Platform fit: Reddit (analytical audience), Instagram (carousel), TikTok (comparison video)
- Engagement driver: Decision-making value, saves

## Behind-the-Scenes Themes

### 13. Team Spotlight
- Introduce team members, their roles, and personalities
- Format: photo, short video, Q&A
- Platform fit: Instagram (feed), TikTok (fun intros), Facebook (community)
- Engagement driver: Human connection, brand personality

### 14. Process Reveals
- Show how your product/service is made or delivered
- Format: time-lapse, walkthrough video, photo series
- Platform fit: TikTok (satisfying process videos), Instagram (Reels), Reddit (detailed breakdown)
- Engagement driver: Curiosity, appreciation for craft

## User-Generated Content Themes

### 15. Community Challenges
- Invite audience to create content around a theme
- Format: hashtag challenge, contest, creative prompt
- Platform fit: TikTok (challenge culture), Instagram (hashtag campaigns), Reddit (contests)
- Engagement driver: Participation, belonging

### 16. Customer Spotlights
- Feature customer content, stories, or creative uses
- Format: repost, interview, compilation
- Platform fit: Instagram (repost), TikTok (duet/stitch), Facebook (share)
- Engagement driver: Community recognition, loyalty

## Seasonal Themes

### 17. Holiday & Event Tie-Ins
- Connect brand to relevant cultural moments and holidays
- Format: themed content, special editions, event coverage
- Platform fit: All platforms (adapt to event type)
- Engagement driver: Timeliness, cultural relevance

### 18. Year-in-Review / Milestone Content
- Celebrate achievements, growth, and milestones
- Format: infographic, video montage, carousel
- Platform fit: Instagram (carousel), Facebook (long post), TikTok (montage)
- Engagement driver: Shared achievement, nostalgia

## Thought Leadership Themes

### 19. Industry Predictions
- Forward-looking analysis and bold predictions
- Format: long-form post, video essay, thread
- Platform fit: Reddit (discussion), Facebook (thought pieces), Instagram (carousel)
- Engagement driver: Discussion, shares, positioning as authority

### 20. Hot Takes & Opinions
- Bold, well-reasoned perspectives on industry topics
- Format: text post, video opinion, carousel
- Platform fit: Reddit (debate culture), TikTok (quick takes), Facebook (discussion)
- Engagement driver: Comments, debates, polarization

## Community Engagement Themes

### 21. Q&A Sessions
- Open the floor to audience questions
- Format: AMA, live Q&A, Stories question sticker, comment threads
- Platform fit: Reddit (AMA), Instagram (Stories Q&A), Facebook (Live), TikTok (LIVE/comments)
- Engagement driver: Direct interaction, community investment

### 22. Polls & Surveys
- Ask audience opinions on relevant topics
- Format: platform-native polls, Stories polls, post-based choices
- Platform fit: Instagram (Stories polls), Facebook (polls), Reddit (poll posts), TikTok (comments vote)
- Engagement driver: Low-effort engagement, data collection

### 23. Collaborative Content
- Co-create content with audience or partners
- Format: duets, stitches, collab posts, community-sourced content
- Platform fit: TikTok (duet/stitch), Instagram (collab), Reddit (community threads)
- Engagement driver: Co-ownership, expanded reach

### 24. Resource Sharing
- Share valuable tools, resources, and recommendations
- Format: link posts, curated lists, tool reviews
- Platform fit: Reddit (resource appreciation), Instagram (carousel lists), Facebook (link posts)
- Engagement driver: Saves, bookmarks, utility value
