# Channel Optimization Guide

## Platform-Audience Fit Scoring Methodology

### Fit Score Formula
The overall platform-audience fit score is calculated as:

```
fitScore = (audienceOverlap × 0.4) + (engagementPotential × 0.35) + (contentFormatMatch × 0.25)
```

Each component is scored 0-1:

### Audience Overlap (Weight: 0.4)
- What percentage of the target audience is active on this platform?
- Use platform demographic data vs. audience segment demographics
- Higher overlap = higher chance of reaching target audience
- Consider both size and concentration of the audience

**Scoring:**
- 0.8-1.0: Target audience is heavily concentrated on this platform
- 0.6-0.8: Significant audience presence
- 0.4-0.6: Moderate audience presence
- 0.2-0.4: Small but reachable audience
- 0.0-0.2: Minimal audience presence

### Engagement Potential (Weight: 0.35)
- What level of engagement can be expected from this audience on this platform?
- Based on industry benchmarks, competitor performance, and platform norms
- Factors: platform's average engagement rate for the industry, audience engagement behavior, content format engagement rates

**Scoring:**
- 0.8-1.0: Audience is highly engaged on this platform (above benchmark)
- 0.6-0.8: Audience engagement meets or exceeds industry average
- 0.4-0.6: Moderate engagement expected
- 0.2-0.4: Below average engagement expected
- 0.0-0.2: Audience is mostly passive on this platform

### Content Format Match (Weight: 0.25)
- How well do the brand's content capabilities match platform content preferences?
- Consider: what the audience prefers, what the platform algorithm favors, what the brand can produce
- Higher scores when brand's natural content style matches platform norms

**Scoring:**
- 0.8-1.0: Brand's content style is native to this platform
- 0.6-0.8: Content can be adapted well to this platform
- 0.4-0.6: Requires significant content adaptation
- 0.2-0.4: Major content style mismatch
- 0.0-0.2: Content style fundamentally incompatible

## Posting Frequency Optimization

### Optimal Frequency Calculation
Posting frequency should balance:
1. **Audience expectations**: How often does the audience want to hear from brands?
2. **Platform norms**: What frequency does the algorithm reward?
3. **Content quality**: Can quality be maintained at this frequency?
4. **Diminishing returns**: At what point does more posting stop adding value?

### Diminishing Returns Analysis
- **Reddit**: 1-3 quality posts/week per subreddit. More than 1/day triggers spam detection.
- **TikTok**: 3-7 posts/week. High-frequency posting rewards discovery but quality must be maintained.
- **Facebook**: 3-5 posts/week for Pages. Groups can tolerate higher frequency.
- **Instagram**: 4-7 feed posts/week + daily Stories. Reels can be more frequent.

### Platform Capacity Planning
For each platform, consider:
- Maximum sustainable posting frequency without quality degradation
- Minimum posting frequency to maintain algorithmic relevance
- Sweet spot: the frequency that maximizes engagement per post

## Cross-Platform Content Coordination

### Coordination Strategies

**Content Cascade**
1. Launch on primary platform first (where audience is strongest)
2. Adapt and publish to secondary platforms within 24-48 hours
3. Use platform-specific formats and language for each adaptation
4. Cross-reference between platforms to drive cross-following

**Platform-Exclusive Strategy**
1. Create unique content for each platform
2. Leverage each platform's strengths (Reddit: depth, TikTok: hooks, Instagram: visuals)
3. Tease exclusive content on other platforms to drive traffic

**Synchronized Campaign**
1. Launch same campaign theme across all platforms simultaneously
2. Adapt format and messaging per platform
3. Use consistent hashtags and campaign names for tracking
4. Time posts to coincide with audience peak hours on each platform

### Content Adaptation Rules
- NEVER cross-post identical content — each platform requires adaptation
- Reddit: Remove all promotional language, lead with value
- TikTok: Reformat as video with trending audio hook
- Facebook: Add discussion prompt for community engagement
- Instagram: Lead with strong visual, add hashtag strategy

## Platform-Specific KPIs

### Reddit KPIs
- Upvote ratio (target: >85%)
- Comment count (engagement depth indicator)
- Save rate (content quality signal)
- Cross-post/share rate (organic amplification)

### TikTok KPIs
- View count and completion rate (primary distribution signal)
- Engagement rate (likes + comments + shares / views)
- Share rate (strongest viral signal)
- Follower growth per post (content quality indicator)

### Facebook KPIs
- Post reach (organic distribution)
- Share count (strongest signal for algorithmic boost)
- Comment threads (engagement depth)
- Group engagement rate (for Group-based strategies)

### Instagram KPIs
- Save rate (strongest positive signal for algorithm)
- Share/send rate (drives Explore page distribution)
- Reel views and completion rate
- Story engagement rate (polls, questions, swipe-ups)
