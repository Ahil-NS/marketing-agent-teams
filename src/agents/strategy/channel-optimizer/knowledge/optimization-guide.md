# Channel Optimization Guide

## Performance Metrics by Platform
- **Reach**: Impressions, views, unique visitors
- **Engagement**: Likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks
- **Conversion**: Click-through rate, sign-ups, purchases
- **Growth**: Follower growth rate, community growth
- **Efficiency**: Cost per engagement, content production effort

## Optimization Signals
- Declining engagement rate despite consistent posting → content refresh needed
- High reach but low engagement → audience targeting mismatch
- Strong engagement on specific formats → double down on those
- Audience growth plateau → channel saturation, consider new channels

## Channel Mix Frameworks
- 80/20 rule: Focus 80% effort on top 2 channels, 20% experimental
- Match channel strength to campaign objective
- Consider audience journey: discovery → engagement → conversion channels
