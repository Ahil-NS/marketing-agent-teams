# Platform-Specific Optimization Strategies

## Reddit Optimization

### Subreddit Selection
- Target subreddits where your audience is active, not just largest subs
- Niche subreddits (10K-500K members) often have higher engagement rates than mega-subs
- Read subreddit rules and sidebar before posting — each community has unique norms
- Monitor subreddit activity patterns to find ideal posting windows
- Build reputation in a subreddit through comments before posting original content

### Karma Building Strategy
- Contribute genuinely helpful comments in target subreddits first (2-4 weeks)
- Share knowledge freely — Reddit rewards generosity with karma and trust
- Upvote and engage with other community members' content
- Post original data, research, or analysis — high-effort content earns high rewards
- Never ask for upvotes — it violates Reddit's rules and community norms

### Community Norms
- Each subreddit has unique culture — study before participating
- Self-promotion rules vary: some allow 10% self-promo, others zero
- Formal vs. casual tone depends on subreddit (r/science vs. r/pics)
- Use Reddit's formatting (headers, bullet points, links) for text posts
- Cross-posting: acceptable if content is genuinely relevant to multiple communities

## TikTok Optimization

### For You Page (FYP) Optimization
- **Watch time:** Single most important metric — keep viewers watching until the end
- **Completion rate:** Videos with high completion rates get amplified
- **Replay rate:** Loop-worthy content gets massive boost
- **Share rate:** Content people send to friends gets distributed wider
- **Comment engagement:** Posts that generate comments rank higher

### Sound/Audio Trends
- Monitor trending sounds weekly through TikTok's Creative Center
- Use trending sounds within 48 hours of emergence for maximum boost
- Original audio can go viral if the content concept is strong
- Mix trending sounds with niche content for discoverability + relevance
- Save trending sounds to use later — they cycle back

### Hashtag Strategy
- 3-5 highly relevant hashtags per post
- Mix broad hashtags (#FYP, #viral) with niche hashtags (#ProductivityHacks)
- Create branded hashtags for campaign tracking
- Research hashtag competition — avoid oversaturated tags
- Use hashtags in caption, not comments (TikTok processes them differently)

## Facebook Optimization

### Group Amplification
- Create branded Groups for community building (deeper engagement than Pages)
- Participate authentically in relevant Groups — don't just promote
- Group posts get higher organic reach than Page posts
- Use Group announcements for important content (pinned visibility)
- Enable and moderate discussions — active Groups get algorithm priority

### Video Priority
- Native video uploads get 10x more reach than YouTube links
- Facebook Reels are now prioritized in the feed
- Live Video gets 6x more engagement than regular video
- Add captions to all video (most Facebook video is watched silently)
- Optimal video length: 1-3 minutes for feed, 3-10 minutes for Watch

### Comment Threading
- Posts that generate comment threads (comment + replies) get algorithm boost
- Ask open-ended questions in posts to encourage discussion
- Reply to every comment within 1 hour to signal active engagement
- Pin thoughtful comments to encourage more discussion
- Avoid engagement bait ("Like if you agree!") — Facebook actively penalizes it

## Instagram Optimization

### Explore Page
- High-quality, niche-specific content has the best chance of Explore
- Consistent use of relevant hashtags helps Instagram categorize your content
- Posts that get saved and shared are more likely to appear on Explore
- Reels have a dedicated tab and frequently appear on Explore
- Engagement velocity (quick engagement after posting) signals Explore-worthy content

### Reels Distribution
- Reels get wider distribution than any other format on Instagram
- Use trending audio for 30-50% of Reels; original audio for the rest
- Optimal Reels length: 15-60 seconds (shorter tends to perform better)
- Text overlays are critical — most Reels are watched without sound
- Hook within first 1-2 seconds is essential for retention

### Carousel Saves
- Carousels have highest save rate of any Instagram format
- Educational carousels (how-to, tips, lists) drive the most saves
- Optimal carousel length: 5-10 slides
- First slide must hook; last slide should have CTA
- Design for swipe momentum — each slide should compel the next swipe

### Stories Best Practices
- 3-5 Stories per day maintains top-of-feed presence
- Use interactive stickers (polls, questions, quizzes) for engagement
- Story engagement boosts your overall profile visibility
- Behind-the-scenes, real-time content works best in Stories
- Add link stickers for conversion-focused Stories
