# Platform Engagement Benchmarks

## Average Engagement Rates by Industry

### Engagement Rate Definitions
- **Reddit**: (upvotes + comments) / unique views (estimated)
- **TikTok**: (likes + comments + shares) / total views
- **Facebook**: (reactions + comments + shares) / reach
- **Instagram**: (likes + comments + saves + shares) / followers

### Industry Benchmark Ranges

| Industry | Reddit | TikTok | Facebook | Instagram |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health & Wellness | 3-8% | 4-12% | 0.5-2% | 1.5-4% |
| Technology/SaaS | 4-10% | 3-8% | 0.3-1.5% | 1-3% |
| Ecommerce/Retail | 2-5% | 5-15% | 0.5-2.5% | 1.5-5% |
| Finance/Fintech | 3-7% | 2-6% | 0.3-1% | 0.8-2.5% |
| Education | 5-12% | 5-10% | 0.5-2% | 1.5-4% |
| Food & Beverage | 3-8% | 8-20% | 1-3% | 2-6% |
| Entertainment | 5-15% | 10-25% | 1-4% | 2-8% |
| B2B Services | 3-8% | 2-5% | 0.3-1% | 0.5-2% |

**Note**: These are general ranges. Niche communities on Reddit and TikTok can dramatically outperform these benchmarks.

## Posting Frequency Sweet Spots

### Reddit
- **Optimal**: 2-4 posts/week across target subreddits
- **Minimum**: 1 post/week to maintain community presence
- **Maximum**: 1 post/day per subreddit (more triggers spam detection)
- **Comments**: 5-10 genuine comments/day builds karma and credibility
- **Key**: Quality and community relevance > frequency

### TikTok
- **Optimal**: 5-7 posts/week (daily or near-daily)
- **Minimum**: 3 posts/week to stay in algorithm rotation
- **Maximum**: 2-3 posts/day (beyond this, posts compete with each other)
- **Key**: Consistency matters more than volume; algorithm rewards active creators

### Facebook
- **Optimal**: 3-5 posts/week for Pages
- **Minimum**: 2 posts/week to maintain reach
- **Maximum**: 1-2 posts/day (overposting suppresses individual post reach)
- **Groups**: Higher frequency acceptable — 1-2 posts/day with community value
- **Key**: Engagement bait is actively penalized; authentic content rewarded

### Instagram
- **Optimal**: 4-7 feed posts/week + daily Stories + 3-5 Reels/week
- **Minimum**: 3 feed posts/week to maintain algorithmic relevance
- **Maximum**: 2 feed posts/day (Grid oversaturation reduces per-post engagement)
- **Stories**: 3-7/day is common for active accounts
- **Key**: Reels frequency can be higher than feed posts without penalty

## Time-of-Day Engagement Patterns

### Reddit Peak Times (US Eastern)
- **Weekdays**: 7-9 AM (morning scroll), 12-2 PM (lunch), 5-7 PM (after work)
- **Weekends**: 9-11 AM (lazy morning), 7-10 PM (evening browsing)
- **Best day**: Monday-Wednesday (higher engagement early in the week)
- **Worst time**: Late night (12-6 AM EST)

### TikTok Peak Times (US Eastern)
- **Weekdays**: 7-9 AM (morning scroll), 12-1 PM (lunch), 7-11 PM (evening)
- **Weekends**: 10 AM - 12 PM, 7-11 PM
- **Best days**: Tuesday, Thursday, Friday (highest engagement)
- **Global**: TikTok's algorithm is timezone-aware; post during target audience's local peak

### Facebook Peak Times (US Eastern)
- **Weekdays**: 9-11 AM (work break), 1-3 PM (afternoon), 7-9 PM (evening)
- **Weekends**: 10 AM - 12 PM, 6-9 PM
- **Best days**: Wednesday, Thursday (highest engagement)
- **Groups**: More flexible timing — community members check Groups at varied times

### Instagram Peak Times (US Eastern)
- **Weekdays**: 7-9 AM (morning), 11 AM - 1 PM (lunch), 7-9 PM (evening)
- **Weekends**: 10 AM - 2 PM, 7-9 PM
- **Best days**: Tuesday, Wednesday (highest engagement for feed posts)
- **Reels**: More flexible — algorithm surfaces good content regardless of posting time
- **Stories**: Post throughout the day; most consumed during commute and evening hours

## Algorithm Preference Signals

### Reddit Algorithm
- **Positive**: High upvote ratio, comment thread depth, save rate, early engagement velocity
- **Negative**: Spam reports, moderation removals, low upvote ratio, excessive self-promotion
- **Critical**: Subreddit rules compliance, authentic participation history, karma

### TikTok Algorithm
- **Positive**: Completion rate (#1), replay rate, share rate, comment depth, save rate
- **Negative**: Skip rate, "not interested" flags, reported content, engagement bait
- **Critical**: First 0.5-1 second hook, watch time retention curve

### Facebook Algorithm
- **Positive**: Shares (#1), meaningful comment threads, reactions (Love > Like), save rate
- **Negative**: Engagement bait, clickbait titles, "angry" reactions clustered, frequently hidden
- **Critical**: Content that generates genuine conversation between friends

### Instagram Algorithm
- **Positive**: Saves (#1), shares/sends, comment depth, story engagement, Reel completion
- **Negative**: Engagement bait, irrelevant hashtags, follow/unfollow patterns, bot activity
- **Critical**: Saves and shares weight more than likes; Reels get separate algorithmic treatment
