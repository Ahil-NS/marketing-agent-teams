# Seasonal Campaign Planning Guide

## Lead Time Planning

### Major Events (4-6 weeks lead time)
- National awareness months (Mental Health Awareness, Pride Month)
- Major holidays (Christmas, Thanksgiving, New Year)
- Industry conferences and events
- Product launches and brand milestones

### Medium Events (2-3 weeks lead time)
- Recognized awareness days/weeks
- Cultural moments (Super Bowl, awards shows)
- Seasonal transitions (back to school, summer kickoff)
- Platform-specific events (Reddit Secret Santa, TikTok challenges)

### Minor Events (1-2 weeks lead time)
- Trending topics and emerging cultural moments
- Weekly recognition days (#ThrowbackThursday, #MotivationMonday)
- Industry-specific dates
- Local/regional events

### Reactive/Real-Time (24-48 hours)
- Breaking news relevant to your industry
- Viral trends and memes
- Competitor actions requiring response
- Unexpected cultural moments

## Content Types Per Event Category

### Awareness Months
- Educational series: Daily/weekly posts exploring the topic
- Personal stories: Authentic sharing from team members or community
- Community challenges: Invite audience participation
- Resource sharing: Helpful links, tools, organizations
- Expert interviews: Feature voices from the awareness community

### Commercial Events (Black Friday, Prime Day)
- Countdown content: Build anticipation over days/weeks
- Deal reveals: Strategic timing of offer announcements
- Gift guides: Curated recommendations by audience segment
- Behind-the-scenes: Show preparation for the event
- User-generated content: Feature customer experiences

### Cultural Moments (Super Bowl, Award Shows)
- Real-time commentary: Quick, witty responses during events
- Prediction content: Pre-event engagement builders
- Recap content: Post-event analysis and highlights
- Trend-riding: Adapt viral moments to your brand niche
- Community discussions: Facilitate conversation around the moment

### Seasonal Transitions
- "Get ready for..." content: Preparation tips, product recommendations
- Aesthetic transitions: Visual content reflecting the new season
- Reflection + forward-looking: What we learned + what's coming
- Seasonal challenges: Community engagement around season themes

## Platform-Specific Seasonal Behavior

### Reddit During Seasonal Events
- Discussion threads perform exceptionally well during events
- AMAs with relevant experts timed to awareness months
- Gift exchange subreddits active during holiday season
- Community polls and surveys around seasonal topics
- Avoid overt commercial content — Reddit users detect and reject it

### TikTok During Seasonal Events
- Branded hashtag challenges tied to events can go viral
- Sound trends often emerge around major events — jump on them fast
- "Day X of [awareness month]" series format drives follow-for-more
- Creative/humorous takes on events spread fastest
- LIVE content during events gets boosted by algorithm

### Facebook During Seasonal Events
- Event pages for brand-hosted gatherings or virtual events
- Community Group discussions around seasonal topics
- Facebook Live during events for real-time engagement
- Fundraiser features for awareness month charity tie-ins
- Photo/memory sharing features popular around holidays

### Instagram During Seasonal Events
- Themed grid aesthetics for seasonal campaigns
- Stories countdown stickers for event anticipation
- Reels with seasonal trending sounds
- Collaborative posts with relevant creators for event content
- Guide/Collection features for curating seasonal content

## Post-Event Follow-Up Strategies

### Within 24 Hours
- Recap and highlight content
- Thank audience for engagement/participation
- Share user-generated content from the event
- Post performance metrics (engagement, reach, participation)

### Within 1 Week
- Detailed analysis and takeaways
- Behind-the-scenes of event preparation and execution
- Lessons learned content
- Community appreciation and spotlight posts

### Long-Term (1-4 Weeks)
- Case study from the campaign
- Planning insights for next time
- Ongoing series that extends event themes
- Audience feedback collection for future events
