# Content Calendar Frameworks

## Rolling Window Scheduling

### 7-Day Rolling Window
- Best for: High-velocity campaigns, trend-riding, time-sensitive promotions
- Planning cadence: Re-plan every 5 days (2-day overlap for continuity)
- Content volume: 15-25 entries across 4 platforms
- Flexibility: High — can pivot quickly based on performance

### 14-Day Rolling Window (Recommended Default)
- Best for: Balanced campaigns, product launches, seasonal pushes
- Planning cadence: Re-plan every 10 days (4-day overlap)
- Content volume: 30-50 entries across 4 platforms
- Flexibility: Medium — allows A/B testing within window

### 30-Day Rolling Window
- Best for: Brand awareness campaigns, content pillars, long-form strategy
- Planning cadence: Re-plan every 25 days (5-day overlap)
- Content volume: 60-120 entries across 4 platforms
- Flexibility: Lower — requires more upfront planning but better thematic coherence

## Content Mix Ratios

### The 80/20 Rule
- 80% value-giving content (educational, entertaining, community-building)
- 20% promotional content (CTAs, offers, product highlights)
- Platform-specific adjustment: Reddit may be 90/10, Instagram 70/30

### Content Pillar Distribution
- Each content pillar should receive 15-30% of total entries
- No single pillar should dominate more than 35% of calendar
- Rotate pillars to avoid audience fatigue
- Track which pillar arrangements generate best engagement

### Content Type Balance
- Educational: 25-35% of entries
- Engagement (community, polls, Q&A): 20-30%
- Behind-the-scenes / storytelling: 15-20%
- Promotional: 10-20%
- Seasonal / reactive: 5-15%

## Publishing Cadence Best Practices

### Consistency Over Volume
- Predictable publishing schedule builds audience expectation
- Missing scheduled posts has more negative impact than low volume
- Start conservative, scale up based on production capacity

### Optimal Spacing
- Spread posts throughout the day (morning, midday, evening)
- Avoid posting on multiple platforms at the same time
- Stagger platform posts by 2-4 hours for cross-platform audiences

### Content Batching
- Group similar content types for production efficiency
- Batch shoot video content, batch design graphics
- Pre-write captions and hashtags during planning phase
- Schedule 1 week of content at a time, leaving room for reactive posts
