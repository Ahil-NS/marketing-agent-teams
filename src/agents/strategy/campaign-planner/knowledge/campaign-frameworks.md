# Campaign Planning Framework

## Campaign Types
- **Launch Campaign**: Product/feature launch with build-up and reveal
- **Awareness Campaign**: Brand/topic awareness with educational focus
- **Engagement Campaign**: Community building and interaction focus
- **Conversion Campaign**: Direct response with clear CTAs
- **Seasonal Campaign**: Time-sensitive around events or seasons

## Campaign Structure
- Pre-launch / Teaser phase (build anticipation)
- Launch / Hero phase (main content push)
- Sustain phase (maintain momentum)
- Optimize phase (iterate based on performance)
- Wrap-up / Recap phase (summarize results)

## Multi-Platform Coordination
- Stagger launches across platforms for sustained reach
- Adapt hero content for platform-specific formats
- Use platform strengths complementarily (awareness on TikTok, depth on Reddit)
- Coordinate hashtags and messaging across platforms
