# Platform Posting Capacity Guidelines

## Reddit Posting Frequency

### Recommended: 1-2 posts per day per subreddit
- **Minimum:** 3-4 posts per week to maintain presence
- **Maximum:** 2 posts per day per subreddit (more triggers spam filters)
- **Comment engagement:** 5-10 comments per day across subreddits is fine
- **Cross-posting limit:** Same content in max 2-3 subreddits

### Reddit Spam Detection Avoidance
- Wait at least 6 hours between posts in the same subreddit
- Vary content types (don't post 3 links in a row)
- Maintain a healthy ratio: at least 9 non-promotional actions per 1 promotional post
- New accounts need 2-4 weeks of engagement before posting original content

### Reddit Timing Notes
- Peak hours: 8-10 AM EST (US audience), 12-2 PM EST
- Monday-Wednesday typically highest engagement
- Weekend traffic varies by subreddit (hobby subs do well, business subs don't)

## TikTok Posting Frequency

### Recommended: 1-3 posts per day
- **Minimum:** 3-5 posts per week (consistency is key)
- **Maximum:** 5 posts per day (diminishing returns after 3)
- **Sweet spot:** 1-2 high-quality posts per day
- **Story/DM engagement:** Unlimited, encouraged

### TikTok Algorithm Considerations
- Posting at consistent times trains the algorithm
- New posts don't compete with your own content (unlike Instagram)
- Each video gets independent distribution based on performance
- First 48 hours are critical for initial distribution

### TikTok Timing Notes
- Peak hours: 7-9 AM, 12-3 PM, 7-11 PM (local audience timezone)
- Tuesday, Thursday, Friday typically highest engagement
- Weekend mornings good for casual/lifestyle content

## Facebook Posting Frequency

### Recommended: 1-2 posts per day
- **Minimum:** 3-5 posts per week
- **Maximum:** 2 posts per day on Page (more than 2 can reduce per-post reach)
- **Groups posting:** 1-3 posts per day per Group
- **Facebook Live:** 1-2 per week maximum

### Facebook Algorithm Considerations
- Quality signals: Meaningful conversations (long comments, comment threads)
- Video gets priority (especially native upload, not YouTube links)
- Posting too frequently can cause algorithm to throttle individual post reach
- Engagement bait (like/share if...) is actively penalized

### Facebook Timing Notes
- Peak hours: 9-11 AM, 1-3 PM (weekdays)
- Wednesday tends to be highest engagement day
- Weekend mornings for community/lifestyle content

## Instagram Posting Frequency

### Recommended: 1-2 feed posts per day + 3-5 Stories per day + 3-7 Reels per week
- **Feed posts minimum:** 3-5 per week
- **Feed posts maximum:** 2 per day (with at least 3 hours between)
- **Stories:** 3-5 per day minimum, up to 10+ if engaging
- **Reels:** 3-7 per week (primary reach driver currently)
- **Carousel:** 2-4 per week (highest save rate)

### Instagram Algorithm Considerations
- Reels distribution: Wider reach than feed posts
- Carousel posts: Highest save rate, strong algorithm signal
- Consistency: Posting at the same times trains follower expectations
- Early engagement: First 30 minutes heavily influence distribution
- Hashtags: 5-15 relevant hashtags per post (avoid banned hashtags)

### Instagram Timing Notes
- Peak hours: 6-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM (local timezone)
- Monday, Wednesday most consistent engagement
- Sunday evening for planning/inspirational content
- Reels: Best posted during high-traffic hours for initial boost
