## Event-to-Content Mapping Strategy

### Lead Time Planning
- Major events: Begin content creation 4-6 weeks before
- Minor events: Begin content creation 1-2 weeks before
- Trending events: React within 24-48 hours

### Content Types Per Event Category
- **Awareness months:** Educational series, personal stories, community challenges
- **Holidays:** Themed visuals, celebratory content, limited offers
- **Cultural moments:** Authentic participation, education, amplification
- **Industry events:** Live coverage, takeaways, commentary

### Platform-Specific Seasonal Behavior
- **Reddit:** Discussion threads about events, community traditions
- **TikTok:** Trend-riding on event hashtags, themed challenges
- **Facebook:** Event pages, community celebrations, family content
- **Instagram:** Themed aesthetics, curated grids, Stories countdowns

### Post-Event Follow-Up
- Recap content performing well 24-48 hours after events
- Behind-the-scenes of event preparation
- Lessons learned / what worked content
- Audience thank-you posts for participation
