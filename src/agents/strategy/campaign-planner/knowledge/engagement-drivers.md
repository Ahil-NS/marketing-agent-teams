# Engagement Drivers by Platform

## Reddit Engagement Drivers

### What Drives Upvotes and Comments
- **Genuine expertise:** Detailed, knowledgeable posts earn respect and engagement
- **Unique data/insights:** Original research, surveys, analysis with sources
- **Asking good questions:** Open-ended questions in the right subreddits
- **Helpful responses:** Answering community questions thoroughly
- **Storytelling:** Personal experience posts with lessons learned

### Optimal Posting Times (EST)
- **Monday-Wednesday:** 8-10 AM, 12-2 PM (highest activity)
- **Thursday-Friday:** 9-11 AM (good for productivity/business subs)
- **Saturday-Sunday:** 10 AM-12 PM (hobby-focused subs peak)

### Content Types by Engagement
1. AMA / expert threads (highest comment engagement)
2. Original analysis with data (highest upvote potential)
3. Detailed how-to guides (highest save/bookmark rate)
4. Discussion/debate starters (highest comment count)
5. Curated resource lists (consistent steady engagement)

## TikTok Engagement Drivers

### What Drives Views and Follows
- **3-second hook:** First 3 seconds determine if viewers stay
- **Trending sounds:** Using popular audio boosts discoverability
- **Text overlays:** 85%+ watch without sound — text is essential
- **Loop potential:** Videos that trick or entice rewatching
- **Emotional triggers:** Surprise, humor, inspiration, relatability

### Optimal Posting Times (Local Timezone)
- **Monday-Wednesday:** 7-9 AM, 12-3 PM (education/business content)
- **Thursday-Friday:** 12-3 PM, 7-11 PM (entertainment peaks)
- **Saturday-Sunday:** 9-11 AM, 7-11 PM (leisure viewing)

### Content Types by Engagement
1. Tutorial/how-to (highest completion rate)
2. Trend-riding with niche twist (highest discoverability)
3. Before/after transformations (highest share rate)
4. Behind-the-scenes authenticity (highest follow conversion)
5. Storytelling series (highest return viewership)

## Facebook Engagement Drivers

### What Drives Meaningful Interactions
- **Conversation starters:** Posts that prompt multi-comment discussions
- **Native video:** 2-5 minute videos with narrative arc
- **Personal stories:** Authentic, relatable personal experiences
- **Community recognition:** Tagging, featuring, or celebrating community members
- **Live video:** Real-time interaction generates 6x more engagement than regular video

### Optimal Posting Times (EST)
- **Monday-Wednesday:** 9-11 AM, 1-3 PM (business content)
- **Thursday-Friday:** 9-11 AM (end-of-week recaps, planning)
- **Saturday-Sunday:** 10 AM-12 PM (community, lifestyle content)

### Content Types by Engagement
1. Facebook Live Q&A (highest real-time engagement)
2. Long-form narrative posts (highest comment depth)
3. Native video with captions (highest reach)
4. Photo albums/carousels (good save/share rate)
5. Group-specific discussions (highest engagement rate)

## Instagram Engagement Drivers

### What Drives Saves, Shares, and Follows
- **Save-worthy content:** Educational carousels, how-to guides, cheat sheets
- **Share-worthy content:** Relatable memes, inspirational quotes, useful tips
- **Visual excellence:** High-quality imagery that stops the scroll
- **Caption storytelling:** Micro-stories that invite comment engagement
- **CTA in every post:** Direct instruction to save, share, comment, or follow

### Optimal Posting Times (Local Timezone)
- **Monday-Wednesday:** 6-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM (highest overall)
- **Thursday-Friday:** 11 AM-1 PM (lunchtime scroll)
- **Saturday-Sunday:** 9-11 AM (weekend lifestyle content)

### Content Types by Engagement
1. Carousel posts (highest save rate — 1.4x over single-image)
2. Reels (highest reach — appears in Explore + Reels tab)
3. Stories with interaction stickers (highest direct engagement)
4. Collab posts (doubled audience, shared engagement)
5. User-generated content reposts (highest trust/authenticity)

## Cross-Platform Engagement Maxims

- **Time content to audience behavior, not arbitrary schedules**
- **First 60 minutes of engagement determine long-term reach on all platforms**
- **Save/bookmark ratio is the most underrated signal — create reference-worthy content**
- **Comments > Likes > Shares in terms of algorithm signal strength (generally)**
- **Respond to comments within 1 hour to boost algorithm placement**
