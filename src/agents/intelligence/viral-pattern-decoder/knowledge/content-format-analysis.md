# Content Format Analysis by Platform

## Format Performance Rankings

Each platform rewards different content formats. This guide ranks formats by
current performance and engagement potential.

## Reddit Best-Performing Formats

### Tier 1 (Highest Engagement)
- **Long-form text posts** — Detailed write-ups, analyses, guides, and stories
  perform exceptionally well in niche subreddits. Reddit audiences value depth.
- **AMAs (Ask Me Anything)** — Direct Q&A format builds authority and drives
  massive comment engagement. Best for establishing expertise.
- **Data visualization + analysis** — Original research, charts, and data-driven
  posts get saved, upvoted, and cross-posted extensively.

### Tier 2 (Strong Engagement)
- **Link posts with context** — Sharing relevant articles/resources with
  personal commentary or summary in the comments.
- **Image posts with captions** — Screenshots, infographics, or photos with
  context in the title. Works well in visual subreddits.
- **Discussion prompts** — Open-ended questions that spark community conversation.

### Tier 3 (Moderate Engagement)
- **Video posts** — Reddit is not a video-first platform, but short informative
  videos can perform well in specific subreddits.
- **Poll posts** — Useful for gauging community opinion, moderate engagement.

### Reddit Format Anti-Patterns
- Obvious self-promotion without contributing value
- Short, low-effort posts in subreddits that value depth
- External links without any context or commentary
- Reposted content without attribution

## TikTok Best-Performing Formats

### Tier 1 (Highest Engagement)
- **Short-form video (15-60 seconds)** — The core TikTok format. Concise,
  hook-driven, high-energy. Completion rate is the #1 ranking signal.
- **Trending sound videos** — Using currently trending audio gets an algorithmic
  boost. The sound is often more important than the visual content.
- **Duets** — Reacting to or building on popular creator content. Inherits some
  of the original video's distribution.

### Tier 2 (Strong Engagement)
- **Stitches** — Using the first 5 seconds of another creator's video as a prompt.
  Great for commentary, reactions, and contrarian takes.
- **Tutorial/how-to videos** — Step-by-step instructional content. Works especially
  well with text overlays and clear visual demonstrations.
- **Storytelling (2-3 minutes)** — Longer narrative content is gaining traction.
  Must maintain engagement throughout (no lulls).

### Tier 3 (Moderate Engagement)
- **Photo carousels** — TikTok's carousel feature (photo mode) is growing. Works
  for educational and lifestyle content.
- **Live streams** — Lower algorithmic distribution but high engagement from
  existing followers.

### TikTok Format Anti-Patterns
- Videos with extended intros before the hook
- Low-quality audio or poor lighting
- Landscape/horizontal format (TikTok is vertical-first)
- Watermarked content from other platforms (Instagram Reels logo, etc.)

## Instagram Best-Performing Formats

### Tier 1 (Highest Engagement)
- **Carousels (education/value)** — Multi-slide posts that deliver value across
  each slide. High save rate and time-on-content. Currently the highest-performing
  organic format on Instagram.
- **Reels (15-60 seconds)** — Instagram's TikTok competitor. Gets massive
  algorithmic distribution to non-followers. Trending audio is key.
- **Collab posts** — Posts co-authored by two accounts. Appears in both feeds,
  doubling potential reach.

### Tier 2 (Strong Engagement)
- **Stories with interactive stickers** — Polls, quizzes, question boxes, and
  sliders drive engagement and signal relevance to the algorithm.
- **Infographic-style posts** — Shareable, saveable, reference-worthy single
  images with data or tips.
- **UGC-style Reels** — Authentic, unpolished content that feels like a friend
  sharing, not a brand advertising.

### Tier 3 (Moderate Engagement)
- **Single-image posts** — Traditional Instagram format, still works but
  lower algorithmic priority compared to Reels and carousels.
- **IGTV/long-form video** — Declining in priority. Better to convert to Reels.
- **Stories (non-interactive)** — Views are declining; interactive elements needed.

### Instagram Format Anti-Patterns
- Over-polished, obviously branded content (authenticity penalty)
- Posting links in captions (Instagram doesn't support clickable caption links)
- Hashtag stuffing in the caption directly (better in comments)
- Posting TikTok-watermarked Reels (algorithmic suppression)

## Facebook Best-Performing Formats

### Tier 1 (Highest Engagement)
- **Native video (1-3 minutes)** — Facebook heavily favors native video uploads
  over external links. Completion rate and comment activity drive distribution.
- **Community posts in Groups** — Group content gets higher News Feed priority
  than Page content. Discussions in active Groups get massive reach.
- **Engagement-driving text posts** — Questions, polls, and discussion starters
  that prompt comments and shares.

### Tier 2 (Strong Engagement)
- **Live video** — Facebook notifies followers and gives algorithmic priority
  to live content during broadcast. Good for Q&A and launches.
- **Photo albums/carousels** — Multiple photos in a single post. Gets good
  engagement when there's a story or progression.
- **Shared user stories** — Third-party validation (testimonials, user stories)
  posted with permission. Drives meaningful comments.

### Tier 3 (Moderate Engagement)
- **Link posts** — Lowest organic reach on Facebook. Algorithm deprioritizes
  posts that send users off-platform.
- **Event posts** — Useful for local businesses, moderate engagement.
- **Single static images** — Basic but functional. Better with strong captions.

### Facebook Format Anti-Patterns
- Engagement bait ("Like if you agree!") — actively penalized by the algorithm
- External link posts without native content
- Cross-posted TikTok/Instagram content without adaptation
- Overly promotional content without community value
