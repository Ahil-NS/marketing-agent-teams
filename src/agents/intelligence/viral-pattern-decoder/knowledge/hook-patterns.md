# Hook Patterns That Drive Engagement

## What Is a Hook?

A hook is the initial attention-grabbing element of content that stops the scroll
and compels the viewer to engage. On social media, you have 0.5-3 seconds to hook
someone before they scroll past. Different platforms reward different hook styles.

## Universal Hook Types

### 1. Curiosity Gap
Creates an information gap that can only be resolved by consuming the content.
- "You won't believe what happened when..."
- "The one thing most people get wrong about..."
- "I've been hiding this for 3 years..."
- **Effectiveness:** High across all platforms
- **Risk:** Can feel clickbaity if content doesn't deliver

### 2. Contrarian Take
Challenges conventional wisdom or popular opinion.
- "Stop doing X — here's why it's hurting you"
- "Hot take: [popular thing] is overrated because..."
- "Everyone says X, but the data shows Y"
- **Effectiveness:** Very high on Reddit and Twitter, high on TikTok
- **Risk:** Must be genuinely defensible, not just provocative

### 3. Relatable Pain Point
Identifies a frustration the audience shares.
- "POV: You just spent 4 hours on something that should take 10 minutes"
- "Why does nobody talk about how hard X is?"
- "If you've ever struggled with X, this is for you"
- **Effectiveness:** High across all platforms, especially TikTok and Instagram
- **Risk:** Must feel authentic, not manufactured

### 4. Transformation / Before-After
Shows dramatic change or results.
- Visual before/after reveal
- "I went from 0 to 100K followers in 6 months — here's how"
- Progress timeline compilations
- **Effectiveness:** Very high on Instagram and TikTok, moderate on Reddit
- **Risk:** Must be credible and verifiable

### 5. Question Hook
Asks a direct question that engages the reader.
- "What's the ONE thing you wish you knew before starting X?"
- "Am I the only one who does this?"
- "How do you handle X situation?"
- **Effectiveness:** High on Facebook and Reddit (drives comments)
- **Risk:** Question must be genuinely interesting

### 6. Statistic / Data Lead
Opens with a surprising or compelling statistic.
- "87% of people get this wrong"
- "The average person wastes 2 hours/day on X"
- "[Specific number] things I learned after [time period] of X"
- **Effectiveness:** High on LinkedIn, Reddit, and Instagram carousels
- **Risk:** Stats must be accurate and sourced

### 7. Pattern Interrupt
Unexpected visual, audio, or textual element that breaks expectations.
- Starting a video with an unrelated dramatic moment
- Using unexpected text overlays
- Sudden tonal shifts
- **Effectiveness:** Very high on TikTok and Instagram Reels
- **Risk:** Must transition smoothly to the actual content

### 8. Social Proof Lead
Opens by establishing credibility or collective validation.
- "After working with 500+ clients, here's what I've learned"
- "This changed my business (and 10K others agree)"
- Showing testimonials or user comments as the opener
- **Effectiveness:** High on Instagram and Facebook
- **Risk:** Can feel self-promotional if not balanced

## Platform-Specific Hook Guidance

### Reddit Hooks
- Title is everything — most viral Reddit posts have curiosity-gap or contrarian titles
- Self-deprecating humor works exceptionally well
- "I built X" or "I analyzed X" signals original content
- Questions in r/AskReddit format drive massive comment engagement
- Data/analysis hooks perform well in niche subreddits

### TikTok Hooks
- First 0.5 seconds must have visual or audio impact
- Text overlays that create curiosity gaps
- Starting with the "result" then explaining how
- "Wait for it..." format (with actual payoff)
- Green screen with reaction to viral content
- #1 mistake most TikTok hooks make: burying the hook after an intro

### Instagram Hooks
- Carousel first slide must be a standalone hook (treated like a headline)
- Reels: same 0.5-1 second rule as TikTok
- Stories: poll/question stickers create interactive hooks
- Caption hooks: first line visible in feed must compel "...more" tap
- Visual hooks: bold colors, large text, unexpected imagery

### Facebook Hooks
- Emotional storytelling opening line
- Personal narrative hooks ("When I was 25, I made the worst decision...")
- Community-oriented hooks ("Who else has experienced X?")
- Video hooks: talking directly to camera, personal tone
- Avoid clickbait — Facebook's algorithm actively penalizes it
