# Viral Mechanics by Platform

## Understanding Virality

Virality is not random — it follows predictable mechanics on each platform. Content
goes viral when it triggers a cascade of engagement signals that the platform's
algorithm interprets as high-quality, relevant content worth distributing more widely.

## Reddit Virality Drivers

### Upvote Cascades
- Reddit's Hot algorithm heavily weights early upvotes — content that gets rapid
  initial engagement gets distributed to more of the subreddit and then r/all
- The first 10 upvotes matter more than the next 100 (logarithmic decay)
- Timing: Posts need engagement within the first 1-2 hours or they die

### Subreddit Amplification
- Cross-posting to related subreddits multiplies exposure
- Comments from subreddit regulars signal quality to the algorithm
- Award/gold signals boost visibility in Hot sort

### Reddit-Specific Viral Patterns
- Contrarian takes that spark debate (high comment counts)
- Personal stories with relatable pain points (emotional engagement)
- Data-driven posts with original analysis (save-worthy)
- Timely responses to breaking news or announcements
- Well-structured AMAs from domain experts

## TikTok Virality Drivers

### For You Page (FYP) Signals
- Completion rate is the #1 signal — videos watched to completion get boosted
- Rewatches (loop count) heavily amplify distribution
- Share-to-like ratio indicates content worth spreading
- Profile visits after watching signal creator authority

### TikTok Batch Testing
- TikTok tests all content with a small batch (300-500 viewers)
- If the batch engagement exceeds thresholds, the next batch is 10x larger
- Each batch must exceed engagement thresholds to get the next distribution round
- This creates exponential growth for truly engaging content

### TikTok-Specific Viral Patterns
- Hook within first 0.5-1 second (before scroll-past)
- Sound/audio trends — using trending sounds gets algorithmic boost
- Pattern interrupts — unexpected visual or audio changes
- Duets and stitches — piggybacking on viral content
- Transformation/before-after reveals
- POV and storytelling formats

## Instagram Virality Drivers

### Instagram Algorithm Signals
- Saves are the highest-value engagement signal (indicates reference-worthy content)
- Shares to Stories and DMs indicate spreadability
- Comment depth (replies to comments) signals genuine conversation
- Carousel completion rate (swiping through all slides)
- Reels completion and rewatch rate (similar to TikTok)

### Instagram-Specific Viral Patterns
- Carousel education posts (swipe for value)
- Reels with trending audio and text overlays
- Collab posts that expose content to both audiences
- Infographic-style content that gets saved for reference
- UGC-style content that feels authentic vs. polished
- Before/after transformations

## Facebook Virality Drivers

### Meaningful Interactions
- Facebook's algorithm prioritizes "meaningful interactions" — comments and shares
  between friends matter more than passive likes
- Share distance — content shared outside the original audience gets boosted
- Comment threading — back-and-forth conversations signal meaningful engagement
- Group sharing — content shared in Groups gets additional algorithmic weight

### Facebook-Specific Viral Patterns
- Emotional storytelling that triggers comments and shares
- Question-based posts that prompt discussion
- Video content with native Facebook upload (not external links)
- Community-oriented content in Groups
- Relatable humor and cultural commentary
- User-generated content and testimonials

## Cross-Platform Viral Mechanics

### Universal Drivers
1. **Emotional resonance** — Content that triggers strong emotions spreads
2. **Social currency** — People share content that makes them look knowledgeable
3. **Practical value** — Useful tips, hacks, and how-tos get saved and shared
4. **Storytelling** — Narrative arc with tension and resolution
5. **Novelty** — Unexpected or surprising information
6. **Identity alignment** — Content that reinforces the sharer's identity

### Engagement Cascade Model
1. **Hook** → Initial attention capture (first impression)
2. **Value delivery** → Content lives up to the hook's promise
3. **Emotional trigger** → Viewer feels compelled to react
4. **Social proof** → Early engagement encourages more engagement
5. **Algorithm boost** → Platform distributes to wider audience
6. **Viral threshold** → Content escapes original audience into general feed
