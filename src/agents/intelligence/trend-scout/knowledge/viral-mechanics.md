# Viral Mechanics Guide

## Universal Virality Drivers
- **Emotional resonance**: Content that triggers strong emotions (awe, humor, outrage, inspiration) spreads faster
- **Identity signaling**: People share content that reflects who they are or want to be
- **Practical value**: Useful tips, hacks, and how-tos earn saves and shares
- **Social currency**: Exclusive info, insider knowledge, or being "first" to share
- **Triggers**: Content tied to everyday cues gets consistent organic reach
- **Stories**: Narrative arcs with tension and resolution drive engagement

## Platform-Specific Virality

### Reddit
- **Authenticity premium**: Overly polished content gets downvoted
- **Community norms**: Each subreddit has unwritten rules — violating them kills reach
- **Timing**: Early upvotes in first 30 minutes determine trajectory
- **Comment engagement**: Posts that spark discussion rise faster than passive consumption

### TikTok
- **Hook in first 2 seconds**: Scroll-stopping openers are mandatory
- **Loop-ability**: Videos that encourage rewatching boost completion rate
- **Sound trends**: Using trending sounds gives algorithmic boost
- **Duet/stitch potential**: Content designed for interaction gets amplified
- **Completion rate**: Most important metric — shorter isn't always better if retention drops

### Instagram
- **Save-worthy content**: Saves signal highest value to algorithm
- **Carousel engagement**: Swipe-through rate on carousels boosts reach
- **Reels algorithm**: Favors original audio, on-screen text, and trending formats
- **Stories interaction**: Polls, quizzes, and question stickers boost profile visibility

### Facebook
- **Share chains**: Content that gets shared to groups has exponential reach
- **Comment depth**: Long comment threads signal high engagement
- **Video autoplay**: First 3 seconds must capture without sound
- **Group dynamics**: Content tailored to specific groups outperforms page posts

## Virality Anti-Patterns
- Clickbait without payoff destroys trust and future reach
- Over-production can signal "ad" and reduce organic engagement
- Ignoring platform norms (e.g., posting TikTok watermark on Reels) gets penalized
- Trend-jacking without relevance feels forced and backfires
