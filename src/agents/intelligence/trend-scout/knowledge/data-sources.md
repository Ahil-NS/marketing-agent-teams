# Trend Data Sources

## Platform-Native Tools
- **Reddit**: r/popular, r/all, subreddit-specific rising/hot feeds
- **TikTok**: Discover page, trending hashtags, Creative Center (trends.tiktok.com)
- **Instagram**: Explore page, Reels trending audio, hashtag pages
- **Facebook**: Trending topics, group activity feeds, Watch trending

## Third-Party Trend Tools
- **Google Trends**: Search volume trends, related queries, regional interest
- **Exploding Topics**: Early-stage trend identification before mainstream
- **SparkToro**: Audience research and affinity data
- **BuzzSumo**: Content performance analysis across platforms
- **Brandwatch / Mention**: Social listening and sentiment tracking

## News and Culture Sources
- **Twitter/X Trending**: Real-time cultural moments
- **Google News**: Breaking stories that might intersect with product domain
- **Hacker News**: Tech and startup trends
- **Product Hunt**: New product launches and innovation trends

## Data Quality Guidelines
- Always cross-reference trends across 2+ sources
- Note data freshness — trends from 7+ days ago may have peaked
- Distinguish between manufactured trends (paid promotion) and organic trends
- Consider geographic scope — a trend in one market may not apply globally
- Track trend lifecycle: emerging → growing → peaking → declining → dead
