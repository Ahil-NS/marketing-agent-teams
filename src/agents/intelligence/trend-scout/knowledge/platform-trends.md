# Platform Trends Research Guide

## Reddit Trend Identification
- Monitor rising posts in relevant subreddits (sort by "Hot" and "Rising")
- Track crosspost frequency as viral indicator
- Analyze comment sentiment for engagement quality
- Watch for AMA requests and community discussions signaling interest shifts
- Key subreddits vary by product domain — identify top 5-10 relevant subs

## TikTok Trend Identification
- Search trending sounds and hashtags related to product domain
- Monitor "For You" page patterns and content format trends
- Track duet/stitch chains as engagement multipliers
- Identify creator archetypes producing viral content in the niche
- Note video length patterns (15s vs 60s vs 3min) by content type

## Instagram Trend Identification
- Monitor Reels trends vs. carousel vs. static post performance
- Track hashtag velocity (rising vs. saturated hashtags)
- Analyze Stories engagement patterns and interactive sticker usage
- Watch for cross-platform content migration (TikTok → Reels)
- Note visual aesthetic trends (color palettes, typography, layout)

## Facebook Trend Identification
- Monitor group discussions for emerging topics
- Track share-to-reaction ratios as virality indicators
- Analyze video completion rates and comment engagement
- Watch for community poll results and discussion threads
- Note content format preferences (video, carousel, text posts)

## Cross-Platform Convergence
- Trends appearing on 2+ platforms simultaneously indicate strong signals
- Time lag between platforms indicates trend direction (usually TikTok → Instagram → Facebook → Reddit)
- Divergent platform performance reveals audience segmentation opportunities
