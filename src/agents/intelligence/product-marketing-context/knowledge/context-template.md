# Product Marketing Context Template

Use this template structure when generating the product marketing context document.

## Required Sections

### Product Overview
- **Product Name**: The official product name
- **Tagline**: One-line value proposition
- **Elevator Pitch**: 2-3 sentence description
- **Key Features**: Top 5 features with benefits
- **Pricing**: Pricing model and tiers
- **Stage**: pre-launch | launched | growth | mature

### Target Audience
- **Demographics**: Age range, location, income, education, occupation
- **Psychographics**: Values, interests, lifestyle, media consumption
- **JTBD**: Primary jobs the product helps with
- **Pain Points**: Top 3-5 frustrations the product addresses
- **Online Presence**: Platforms, communities, content preferences

### Customer Personas
For each persona (2-3):
- **Name**: A representative name
- **Role**: Professional title or life role
- **Age**: Representative age
- **Goals**: What they want to achieve
- **Challenges**: What stands in their way
- **Preferred Platforms**: Where they engage
- **Content Preferences**: What formats they consume
- **Quote**: A representative statement in their voice

### Brand Voice & Tone
- **Personality Traits**: 3-5 adjective descriptors
- **Tone Spectrum**: Position on formal↔casual and serious↔playful axes
- **Do Say**: 5+ example phrases/approaches that match the brand
- **Don't Say**: 5+ example phrases/approaches to avoid
- **Grammar Preferences**: Oxford comma, em dashes, exclamation points, emoji usage

### Competitive Landscape
- **Competitors**: Name, positioning, strengths, weaknesses
- **Differentiators**: What makes this product unique
- **Positioning Statement**: "For [audience] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [competitor], [product] [differentiator]."

### Proof Points
- **Testimonials**: Direct quotes with attribution
- **Metrics**: Key stats (users, growth, satisfaction)
- **Social Proof**: Ratings, reviews, community size
- **Credentials**: Awards, press, certifications

### Marketing Goals
- **Primary Objective**: awareness | leads | conversions | retention
- **Key Metrics**: Specific measurable targets
- **Timeline**: Campaign or quarter-level milestones
- **Constraints**: Budget, compliance, brand guidelines

### Content Preferences
- **Formats**: Ranked preference (short-form social, long-form blog, video, infographic)
- **Topics**: Focus areas and content pillars
- **Avoid**: Topics, claims, or approaches to steer clear of
- **Compliance**: Industry-specific regulations to follow
