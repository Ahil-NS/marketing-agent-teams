# Platform Demographic Data & Usage Patterns

## Reddit Demographics

### Core Demographics
- **Age**: Skews 18-49, with heaviest concentration in 18-29 (36%) and 30-49 (34%)
- **Gender**: Male-skewing (~63% male, ~37% female), though this varies significantly by subreddit
- **Education**: Higher than average — overindexes for college-educated users
- **Income**: Broad distribution, slight overindex for middle-to-upper-middle income ($50K-$150K)
- **Geography**: Primarily US (~48%), UK, Canada, Australia, India; heavily English-speaking
- **Tech Affinity**: High — Reddit users are early adopters and tech-savvy

### Usage Patterns
- **Session Duration**: Long sessions (15-30 min average), deep content consumption
- **Content Consumption**: Text-heavy, values depth over polish; prioritizes substance and authenticity
- **Engagement Style**: Comment-heavy platform — discussions and threads are the core behavior
- **Content Creation**: Low creator-to-consumer ratio; most users are lurkers (90%+ read-only)
- **Discovery**: Within subreddits via sort (hot/new/top); cross-subreddit via home feed
- **Anonymity**: Pseudonymous — users are more honest and direct than other platforms
- **Trust Signals**: Karma, account age, post history, verified flair

### Content Consumption Habits
- Text posts with data, analysis, or personal stories perform best
- AMAs (Ask Me Anything) drive massive engagement in relevant communities
- Visual content must add genuine value — no Instagram-style polished aesthetics
- Humor and memes perform well but must be community-appropriate
- Commercial content is actively rejected unless genuinely helpful

## TikTok Demographics

### Core Demographics
- **Age**: Gen Z (18-24: ~38%) and young Millennials (25-34: ~32%); growing 35+ segment
- **Gender**: Slightly female-skewing (~57% female, ~43% male)
- **Income**: Broad; younger users often lower-income but high purchasing influence
- **Geography**: Global reach, localized algorithm — content surfaces to local audiences
- **Tech Affinity**: Mobile-native; nearly 100% mobile usage

### Usage Patterns
- **Session Duration**: High (average 95 minutes/day); algorithm-driven endless scroll
- **Content Consumption**: Discovery-driven — users don't follow, they discover via For You Page
- **Engagement Style**: Passive consumption with bursts of creation; duets, stitches, comments
- **Content Creation**: Higher creator ratio than other platforms; low barrier to content creation
- **Discovery**: Algorithm-first (FYP), not follower-first — content quality matters more than follower count
- **Attention Span**: First 0.5-3 seconds are critical; content must hook immediately

### Content Consumption Habits
- Short-form video (15-60 seconds) is primary format; 3-10 minute content growing
- Trending sounds and audio are essential for discoverability
- Authentic, unpolished content outperforms slick production
- Educational content ("learn something new") performs well across age groups
- Entertainment-first with value embedded performs better than educational-first
- Carousel/photo mode gaining traction for informational content

## Facebook Demographics

### Core Demographics
- **Age**: Skews 35+; strongest in 35-54 (34%) and 55+ (22%); declining among 18-24
- **Gender**: Roughly even split (~52% female, ~48% male)
- **Income**: Broad distribution, strong middle-income representation
- **Education**: Broad distribution, slightly less education-skewed than Reddit/LinkedIn
- **Geography**: Global — largest platform by country coverage; strong in developing markets
- **Tech Affinity**: Moderate — comfortable with tech but not early adopters

### Usage Patterns
- **Session Duration**: Moderate (30-50 min/day); check-in behavior throughout the day
- **Content Consumption**: Community-oriented; Groups and Pages drive significant engagement
- **Engagement Style**: Reactions, shares, and comments; less likely to create original content
- **Content Creation**: Low original creation; heavy sharing/resharing behavior
- **Discovery**: Algorithm-curated feed prioritizing friend/family content and Groups
- **Community**: Facebook Groups are the platform's strongest engagement feature

### Content Consumption Habits
- Video content (especially Reels) gets highest organic reach
- Community discussions in Groups drive deep engagement
- Event-based content and local/community content performs well
- Long-form text posts can work if they tell a compelling story
- Shares are the strongest signal — content people forward to friends
- Live video gets algorithmic boost and notification priority

## Instagram Demographics

### Core Demographics
- **Age**: 18-34 is primary (62%); 25-34 is single largest cohort (~32%)
- **Gender**: Slightly female-skewing (~51% female, ~49% male)
- **Income**: Overindexes for higher income ($75K+) and urban professionals
- **Education**: Skews college-educated
- **Geography**: Global with strong US, India, Brazil, Indonesia presence
- **Tech Affinity**: High mobile proficiency; visual-first orientation

### Usage Patterns
- **Session Duration**: Moderate-high (30-45 min/day); split between feed, Stories, Reels, Explore
- **Content Consumption**: Visual-first; aesthetics and production quality matter more than other platforms
- **Engagement Style**: Likes, saves, shares, story replies; DMs are a major engagement channel
- **Content Creation**: Moderate creator ratio; Stories are easiest creation format
- **Discovery**: Hashtags, Explore page, Reels tab; suggested content via algorithm
- **Shopping**: Shopping-integrated — direct purchase behavior from content

### Content Consumption Habits
- Reels are now the highest-reach format (algorithmic priority)
- Carousels drive highest save rate — educational and step-by-step content
- Stories maintain engagement between main posts; polls and questions drive interaction
- Aesthetic quality matters — polished visuals outperform raw content (opposite of TikTok)
- Strong hashtag ecosystem — 3-5 highly relevant hashtags per post
- Saves and shares are stronger signals than likes for algorithm distribution

## Cross-Platform Audience Behavior

### Multi-Platform Usage Patterns
- Most audiences use 4-7 social platforms but have 2-3 "primary" platforms
- Content consumption patterns differ by platform even for the same person
- Same user may be a lurker on Reddit but creator on TikTok
- Platform choice correlates with content intent: entertainment (TikTok), community (Reddit), visual inspiration (Instagram), connection (Facebook)

### Platform Selection by Content Intent
| Intent | Primary Platform | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Entertainment | TikTok | Instagram Reels |
| Learning | Reddit, YouTube | TikTok (quick tips) |
| Community | Reddit, Facebook Groups | Discord |
| Visual Inspiration | Instagram | Pinterest |
| News/Updates | Reddit, Twitter/X | Facebook |
| Shopping | Instagram | TikTok Shop |
| Professional | LinkedIn | Reddit (industry subs) |

### Cross-Platform Content Adaptation
- Same content concept must be formatted differently per platform
- TikTok: video-first, hook-driven, trending audio
- Reddit: text-first, data-driven, community-appropriate
- Instagram: visual-first, aesthetic, hashtag-optimized
- Facebook: community-first, shareable, discussion-generating
