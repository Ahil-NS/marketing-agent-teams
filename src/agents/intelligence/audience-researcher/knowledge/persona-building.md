# Persona Building Guide

## Data-Driven Persona Construction

### What Makes a Good Persona
- Based on real behavioral data, not assumptions or stereotypes
- Specific enough to guide content decisions (not "millennials who like fitness")
- Includes observable behaviors that content creators can target
- Maps directly to content strategy decisions (format, tone, platform, topics)
- Represents a meaningful segment of the total audience (not a niche of a niche)

### Persona Template Structure
Each persona should include:
1. **Name**: A memorable, descriptive name (e.g., "Wellness-Seeking Sarah", "Data-Driven Dave")
2. **Age Range**: Specific enough to guide content tone (e.g., "28-35", not "young adult")
3. **Segment**: Which audience segment this persona represents
4. **Key Demographics**: 3-5 defining demographic traits
5. **Psychographic Profile**: VALS type, values, motivations, lifestyle
6. **Primary Platforms**: Top 2-3 platforms ranked by usage
7. **Content Preferences**: Formats, topics, and tones that resonate
8. **Pain Points**: Top 3-5 pain points with specificity
9. **Behavioral Indicators**: Observable digital behaviors
10. **Messaging Angle**: The most effective way to reach this persona

## Behavioral Indicators

### What Are Behavioral Indicators?
Observable actions and patterns that signal a person belongs to a particular persona. These help content creators target the right audience with the right content.

### Types of Behavioral Indicators

**Content Consumption Indicators**
- Follows specific accounts or subscribes to specific subreddits
- Saves/bookmarks educational or inspirational content
- Watches videos to completion on specific topics
- Reads long-form articles in specific categories
- Uses specific hashtags when posting

**Purchase Intent Indicators**
- Searches for product comparisons or reviews
- Joins communities dedicated to the product category
- Asks questions about products in forums or comment sections
- Follows brand accounts and engages with product content
- Uses shopping features on Instagram or TikTok

**Engagement Indicators**
- Comments regularly on content (active engager)
- Shares content to personal networks (amplifier)
- Creates user-generated content (creator)
- Participates in polls, quizzes, and interactive content (interactive)
- Lurks but saves content for later reference (passive researcher)

**Platform-Specific Indicators**
- Reddit: Subscribes to niche subreddits, has moderate karma, comments regularly
- TikTok: Likes trending sounds, follows niche creators, watches full videos
- Facebook: Active in Groups, shares articles, engages with community posts
- Instagram: Saves posts, engages with Stories polls, uses shopping features

## Content Affinity Mapping

### How to Map Content Affinity
For each persona, map their affinity to:
1. **Content Format**: Which formats they prefer (video, text, carousel, infographic)
2. **Content Tone**: Which tones resonate (educational, entertaining, inspirational, conversational)
3. **Content Topics**: Which subjects they care about most
4. **Content Length**: Preferred duration/length (15s video vs. 3min, tweet-length vs. essay)
5. **Content Source**: Who they trust (experts, peers, brands, influencers)

## Platform Behavior Profiling

### Per-Persona Platform Behavior
For each persona, document:
- **Primary platform**: Where they spend the most time
- **Usage intent**: Why they use this platform (entertainment, learning, community, shopping)
- **Engagement type**: How they interact (create, comment, share, lurk)
- **Peak activity times**: When they're most active on this platform
- **Content discovery**: How they find new content (algorithm, search, community, hashtags)

### Platform Behavior Profiles

**Reddit Profile Types**
- **The Researcher**: Subscribes to niche subreddits, reads deeply, rarely posts but saves extensively
- **The Commenter**: Regular contributor in discussions, has moderate karma, values being helpful
- **The Creator**: Posts original content (analyses, guides, AMAs), high karma, community recognized
- **The Lurker**: Reads and upvotes only, no comments or posts, uses Reddit for passive consumption

**TikTok Profile Types**
- **The Scroller**: Passive FYP consumer, engages via likes, rarely comments or creates
- **The Trendjumper**: Creates content using trending sounds and formats, chases virality
- **The Niche Creator**: Consistent content in a specific niche, engaged community, moderate following
- **The Explorer**: Uses search and hashtags to find specific content, saves tutorials and how-tos

**Facebook Profile Types**
- **The Community Member**: Active in Groups, provides and receives advice, values relationships
- **The Sharer**: Rarely creates original content, but shares articles, memes, and updates frequently
- **The Observer**: Checks feed regularly but rarely engages, uses Marketplace and Events

**Instagram Profile Types**
- **The Aesthete**: Curates a beautiful feed, follows aesthetically pleasing accounts, high save rate
- **The Story Consumer**: Primarily watches Stories, votes in polls, sends DM replies, skips main feed
- **The Shopper**: Uses Instagram for product discovery, taps shopping tags, saves product posts
- **The Reel Scroller**: Primarily consumes Reels, similar behavior to TikTok scrolling

## Pain Point Taxonomy

### Categories of Pain Points
1. **Functional Pain Points**: Things that don't work well or take too long
2. **Financial Pain Points**: Cost-related frustrations, perceived waste, value gaps
3. **Process Pain Points**: Inefficiencies, friction, unnecessary complexity
4. **Support Pain Points**: Lack of help, poor documentation, unanswered questions
5. **Social Pain Points**: Feeling left out, not fitting in, social pressure
6. **Emotional Pain Points**: Stress, anxiety, frustration, overwhelm
7. **Knowledge Pain Points**: Not knowing how, lack of information, confusion

### Pain Point Severity Scoring
- **High**: Affects daily life/work, actively searching for solutions, willing to pay to solve
- **Medium**: Noticeable frustration, would switch to a better solution if convenient, mentions in discussions
- **Low**: Minor annoyance, not actively seeking solutions, but appreciates when addressed
