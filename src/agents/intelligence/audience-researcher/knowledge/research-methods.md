# Audience Research Methods

## Demographic Research
- Age, gender, location, income, education level
- Professional background and industry
- Life stage and family status

## Psychographic Analysis
- Values, beliefs, and attitudes
- Lifestyle preferences and hobbies
- Pain points and aspirations
- Media consumption habits

## Platform Behavior Patterns
- Primary vs. secondary platform usage
- Content interaction patterns (passive vs. active)
- Peak activity times by platform
- Device usage patterns (mobile vs. desktop)

## Community Mapping
- Identify relevant subreddits, Facebook groups, Discord servers
- Estimate community size and engagement rates
- Note community culture and norms
- Track influential voices and thought leaders
