# TikTok For You Page Algorithm

## How TikTok Distribution Works

TikTok's algorithm is uniquely powerful because it distributes content primarily
based on content quality signals rather than follower count. Even new accounts
with zero followers can go viral if the content triggers the right signals.

## The Batch Testing Model

### How It Works
1. **Initial batch:** Every new video is shown to a small test audience (300-500 users)
2. **Signal evaluation:** TikTok measures engagement signals from this batch
3. **Escalation decision:** If signals exceed thresholds, the video gets a larger batch (5K-50K)
4. **Continued escalation:** Each successful batch triggers a 5-10x larger batch
5. **Viral threshold:** If content consistently passes batch tests, it reaches millions

### Batch Progression (Approximate)
- Batch 1: ~300-500 views
- Batch 2: ~5,000-10,000 views
- Batch 3: ~50,000-100,000 views
- Batch 4: ~500,000-1,000,000 views
- Batch 5+: Millions (true viral territory)

### Key Insight
Content quality matters more than account size. A video from a 0-follower account
that performs well in Batch 1 can outperform a video from a 1M-follower account
that underperforms in its initial batch.

## Primary Ranking Signals

### Tier 1 (Strongest Signals)
1. **Completion rate** — What percentage of viewers watch the video to the end?
   This is widely considered TikTok's #1 ranking signal. Shorter videos have a
   natural advantage (easier to complete).
2. **Rewatch rate** — How many viewers watch the video more than once? Rewatches
   are a very strong positive signal.
3. **Share rate** — Shares to friends, other platforms, or as DMs indicate
   content worth spreading.

### Tier 2 (Strong Signals)
4. **Comment rate** — Comments indicate active engagement. Comments that mention
   friends (@mentions) are especially valuable.
5. **Like rate** — Likes are a positive signal but weaker than completion, shares,
   or comments.
6. **Profile visits** — Viewers clicking to the creator's profile after watching
   signals high interest.
7. **Follow rate** — New follows from the FYP indicate strong creator appeal.

### Tier 3 (Supporting Signals)
8. **Save/bookmark rate** — Users saving the video for later reference.
9. **Sound usage** — Other creators using the same sound creates a viral sound
   ecosystem.
10. **Stitch/Duet rate** — Other creators building on the content.

## Content Diversity Factor

TikTok actively diversifies a user's FYP to prevent content bubbles:
- The algorithm mixes in content from new creators periodically
- Content categories rotate to prevent user fatigue
- "Not interested" signals are weighted heavily to avoid repetition

## Creator Score

TikTok maintains an internal creator score based on:
- Consistency of posting (regular posting is rewarded)
- Average engagement rate across recent videos
- Content quality trends (improving or declining)
- Community guideline compliance
- Live streaming activity
- Creator tool usage (effects, trending sounds)

## Sound/Audio Impact

### Trending Sounds
- Using a currently trending sound gets an algorithmic distribution boost
- The TikTok creative center shows trending sounds
- Sound trends have a lifecycle: emerging (~1-3 days), peak (~3-7 days), declining

### Original Sounds
- Original audio that gets reused by others creates a flywheel effect
- The creator of a trending sound gets additional visibility
- Original sounds are valuable for brand identity but sacrifice trending sound boost

## Anti-Patterns (What Suppresses Distribution)

1. **Watermarks from other platforms** — Instagram Reels logo, YouTube watermark
2. **Low video quality** — Blurry, poorly lit, or low-resolution videos
3. **Community guideline violations** — Even soft violations reduce distribution
4. **Spam-like behavior** — Posting identical content, follow/unfollow spam
5. **Excessive hashtag use** — Using 15+ hashtags looks spammy
6. **Clickbait without delivery** — Hook that doesn't match content
7. **Political/sensitive content** — May not violate guidelines but gets reduced distribution
8. **Horizontal video format** — TikTok is vertical-first; landscape videos underperform

## Recent Algorithm Trends (Track These)

- Longer content (2-3 minutes) is gaining distribution as TikTok competes with YouTube
- Search functionality is becoming a distribution channel (SEO in captions/hashtags)
- STEM and educational content is getting explicit algorithmic support
- Creator monetization programs influence how the algorithm rewards certain content types
- TikTok Shop integration affects content distribution for e-commerce creators
