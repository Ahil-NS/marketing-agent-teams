# Instagram Algorithm Mechanics

## Instagram's Multi-Algorithm System

Instagram doesn't use one algorithm — it uses multiple ranking systems across
different surfaces (Feed, Explore, Reels, Stories). Each surface has its own
ranking criteria.

## Feed Algorithm

### Primary Ranking Signals
1. **Relationship** — How close the viewer is to the creator (DMs, profile visits,
   comments, tagged photos). Closer relationships = higher priority.
2. **Interest** — How likely the viewer is to engage with this content type based
   on past behavior.
3. **Timeliness** — More recent posts rank higher (though not strictly chronological).
4. **Frequency** — How often the viewer opens Instagram affects how curated their feed is.
5. **Following count** — Users who follow many accounts see a more curated/competitive feed.

### Feed Content Type Weighting
- Reels currently get strong Feed distribution (Instagram pushing video)
- Carousels get extended distribution (multiple surfaces over time)
- Single images get standard distribution
- Link-containing posts get slightly suppressed (Instagram wants users to stay)

## Explore Page Algorithm

### How Content Gets to Explore
1. **Seed signal:** Content performs well with the creator's existing audience
2. **Expansion:** Algorithm finds "look-alike" audiences based on interest signals
3. **Explore feed:** Content is tested with Explore users; if engagement is good,
   it gets wider Explore distribution

### Explore Ranking Signals
1. **Engagement velocity** — How fast the content gained likes, comments, saves, shares
2. **Save rate** — Explore heavily weights saves (indicates reference-worthy content)
3. **Share rate** — Shares indicate content worth spreading to others
4. **Content freshness** — Recent content prioritized over older content
5. **Creator history** — Creators who consistently produce Explore-worthy content get favored

## Reels Algorithm

### Distribution Mechanics
- Reels has its own dedicated distribution system separate from Feed
- Reels can reach non-followers (major growth channel)
- Instagram is actively pushing Reels to compete with TikTok

### Reels Ranking Signals
1. **Completion rate** — Watch-through percentage (similar to TikTok)
2. **Audio usage** — Trending audio gets a distribution boost
3. **Engagement rate** — Likes, comments, shares relative to views
4. **Originality** — Original content preferred over reposts/watermarked content
5. **Visual quality** — Well-lit, high-resolution, vertical format preferred

### Reels Anti-Patterns
- Visibly watermarked content from TikTok or other platforms
- Low-resolution or blurry video
- Heavily text-only Reels without visual interest
- Recycled content that Instagram can detect as non-original

## Stories Algorithm

### Stories Ranking
- Stories from accounts you interact with most appear first
- Stories with interactive stickers (polls, questions) get priority
- Frequent Story posters maintain visibility (daily is ideal)
- Stories with music get slight boost

### Stories Best Practices
- Post 3-7 Stories per day for optimal visibility
- Use interactive stickers (polls, questions, quizzes) in at least 1 per batch
- Respond to Story replies — this strengthens relationship signals
- Use location and hashtag stickers for discoverability

## Engagement Signal Weighting

### Highest Value Signals
1. **Saves** — The #1 most valuable engagement signal on Instagram. Indicates content
   is reference-worthy and will be revisited.
2. **Shares (via DM or Stories)** — Content shared privately or to Stories indicates
   high social value.
3. **Comments (substantive)** — Genuine comments > emoji reactions. Comment conversations
   (replies) are especially valuable.

### Moderate Value Signals
4. **Profile visits** — Viewer clicking to profile after seeing content
5. **Follows** — New follows from content indicate strong appeal
6. **Time spent** — How long users spend on the content

### Lower Value Signals
7. **Likes** — Still positive but the weakest engagement signal
8. **Story views** — Passive viewing without interaction

## Hashtag Strategy

### Current Best Practices
- 5-15 hashtags per post (optimal range varies, test for your account)
- Mix of broad (1M+ posts), medium (100K-1M), and niche (<100K) hashtags
- Place hashtags in the first comment rather than caption for cleaner aesthetics
- Hashtag effectiveness has declined compared to years past — content quality matters more
- Avoid banned or shadowbanned hashtags

### Hashtag Decay
- Hashtags have a diminishing effectiveness curve — using the same hashtags repeatedly
  reduces their distribution impact
- Rotate hashtag sets across posts
- Research fresh hashtags relevant to current trends

## Saves-to-Likes Ratio

A high saves-to-likes ratio is one of the strongest signals Instagram uses to
determine content quality. Content that people save but don't necessarily like
(educational, reference, tutorial) gets strong algorithmic boost. Target > 2%
save rate for optimal distribution.

## Collab Posts

- Collab posts appear in both creators' feeds
- Engagement is shared/combined, boosting overall algorithmic signal
- Excellent for reaching new audiences without paid promotion
- Both collaborators must approve the post before publishing
