# Competitive Analysis Framework

## Analysis Dimensions
- Content strategy (formats, themes, frequency)
- Platform presence and channel mix
- Messaging and positioning
- Audience engagement patterns
- Visual identity and brand consistency
- Paid vs. organic strategy indicators

## Benchmarking Methods
- Track posting frequency by platform
- Estimate engagement rates (likes, comments, shares / followers)
- Analyze content mix ratios (educational, promotional, entertainment)
- Monitor hashtag strategies and keyword targeting
- Note influencer partnerships and collaborations

## Gap Identification
- Content formats competitors aren't using
- Audience segments not being targeted
- Platform opportunities being ignored
- Messaging angles not being leveraged
- Timing gaps in posting schedules
