# Competitive Analysis Frameworks

## SWOT Analysis
Apply SWOT to each competitor's marketing presence:
- **Strengths**: What are they doing well? (engagement rates, content quality, community, brand voice consistency)
- **Weaknesses**: Where are they falling short? (inconsistent posting, poor engagement, missing platforms, weak CTAs)
- **Opportunities**: What gaps exist? (underserved audience segments, unused content formats, untapped platforms)
- **Threats**: What competitive advantages could erode the client's position? (growing competitor audiences, viral competitor content, aggressive paid strategies)

## Content Gap Analysis
1. **Format gaps**: Content formats competitors aren't using (carousels, tutorials, user stories, AMAs)
2. **Topic gaps**: Subject areas with audience demand but no competitor coverage
3. **Platform gaps**: Platforms where audience exists but competitors are absent or weak
4. **Timing gaps**: Posting schedules that leave audience underserved at specific times
5. **Sentiment gaps**: Audience pain points mentioned in comments but not addressed by competitor content

## Engagement Benchmarking
### Metrics to Track Per Platform
- **Engagement rate**: (likes + comments + shares) / followers × 100
- **Posting frequency**: Posts per week/month
- **Content mix ratio**: % educational / % promotional / % entertainment / % UGC
- **Response rate**: How often and how quickly competitors reply to comments
- **Growth rate**: Follower growth over past 30/60/90 days (estimated)

### Benchmarking Categories
| Category | Low | Average | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram engagement | <1% | 1-3% | >3% |
| TikTok engagement | <3% | 3-8% | >8% |
| Reddit post upvotes | <50 | 50-500 | >500 |
| Facebook engagement | <0.5% | 0.5-2% | >2% |

## Competitive Positioning Map
Position competitors along two axes:
- **X-axis**: Content sophistication (basic → premium production)
- **Y-axis**: Community engagement (broadcast-only → deep interaction)
Identify the quadrant with the most white space for client positioning.

## Share of Voice Estimation
- Count competitor mentions across platforms using web search
- Compare relative posting volume and engagement levels
- Estimate mindshare based on community discussions and brand mentions
- Track trending competitor hashtags vs. client brand hashtags
