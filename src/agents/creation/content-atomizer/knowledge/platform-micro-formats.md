# Platform Micro-Content Formats

## Reddit

### Thread Format
- Numbered posts with clear section headers
- TL;DR at the top or bottom (convention varies by subreddit)
- Key takeaways as bullet points
- 3000-10000 character ideal length for text posts
- Discussion question at the end to drive engagement
- Use Reddit markdown formatting (bold, italic, headers, lists)

### Discussion Starters
- Open-ended questions that invite personal experience sharing
- "What's your experience with..." format
- Contrarian takes that challenge conventional wisdom
- "Unpopular opinion:" prefix for controversial angles

### AMA-Style Q&A
- Pre-formatted question-and-answer pairs
- Use bold for questions, regular text for answers
- Include "proof" or credibility markers
- Keep responses conversational and authentic

### Character Limits & Constraints
- Title: 300 characters max
- Body: 40,000 characters max
- Flair required in many subreddits
- No direct links in body for many subreddits (text posts preferred)

## TikTok

### Script Hook Formats
- "3 things I learned about [topic]" — numbered list with punchy reveals
- "Stop scrolling if you [qualifier]" — direct address with relevance hook
- "Nobody talks about [hidden truth]" — curiosity gap opener
- "POV: you just [scenario]" — relatable situation framing
- React/response to trending content — stitch or duet format

### Script Structure
- Hook: 0-3 seconds (must stop the scroll)
- Setup: 3-8 seconds (context and promise)
- Value delivery: 8-25 seconds (core content)
- CTA: last 3-5 seconds (follow, save, share)
- Total: 15s, 30s, or 60s

### Caption & Hashtag Rules
- Caption: up to 2200 characters
- 4-6 hashtags: mix of trending + niche + brand
- Keywords in first line for search discoverability
- CTA in caption reinforcing the video CTA

## Facebook

### Key Insight Posts
- Lead with the most surprising or valuable insight
- 1-3 paragraphs max, with line breaks between
- End with a question to prompt comments
- Optimal length: 40-80 words for engagement

### Question Posts
- Simple, direct question format
- "What's your take on...?" or "Have you ever...?"
- Avoid yes/no questions — open-ended drives more engagement
- Keep under 100 characters for highest engagement

### Carousel Summaries
- 3-5 slides summarizing key points from long-form content
- One key takeaway per slide
- Visual consistency across slides
- CTA on final slide

### Video Teasers
- 15-30 second video teaser from longer content
- Text overlay with key insight
- End card with CTA to read full content
- Optimal for News Feed placement

### Character Limits & Constraints
- Post text: 63,206 characters max (but optimal is under 250)
- Image posts: 1200x630px recommended
- Video: up to 240 minutes
- Stories: 20 seconds per frame

## Instagram

### Carousel Slides
- Hook slide: Bold statement or question that compels swipe
- Content slides: 1 key point per slide, clean typography
- Evidence slide: Stats, quotes, or proof
- CTA slide: Follow, save, share, or link in bio
- Optimal: 5-10 slides per carousel
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 (feed) or 4:5 (portrait, preferred)

### Caption Extraction
- Open with a hook that matches the visual
- Break into paragraphs with line breaks
- Include relevant hashtags (20-25)
- CTA at the end (save this, share with a friend, comment below)
- Optimal caption length: 138-150 characters for engagement (or 2000+ for storytelling)

### Reels Hooks
- Pattern interrupt in first 2 seconds
- Text overlay immediately visible
- Trending audio for algorithmic boost
- "Save this for later" content performs best
- Aspect ratio: 9:16

### Character Limits & Constraints
- Caption: 2200 characters max
- Hashtags: 30 max (20-25 recommended)
- Stories: 60 seconds (or split across frames)
- Reels: up to 90 seconds
- Bio link for traffic driving
