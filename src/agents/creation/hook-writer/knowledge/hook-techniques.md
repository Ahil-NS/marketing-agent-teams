# Hook Writing Techniques

## 12+ Hook Archetypes

### 1. Question Hook
Opens with a question that creates a knowledge gap the reader must close.
- Reddit: "Has anyone else noticed that [specific observation]?" (avg 2.3x more comments)
- TikTok: "Why does nobody talk about this?" (drives completion rate)
- Facebook: "What would you do if [relatable scenario]?" (drives comments)
- Instagram: "Want to know the secret behind [desirable outcome]?" (drives saves)

### 2. Statistic Hook
Leads with a surprising number or data point that challenges assumptions.
- Reddit: "[87% of people] don't realize [counterintuitive fact] — here's the data" (authority)
- TikTok: "Only 3% of people know this trick" (curiosity + exclusivity)
- Facebook: "We surveyed 1,000 people and the #1 answer was..." (social proof)
- Instagram: "This one change increased [metric] by 340%" (save-worthy)

### 3. Story Hook
Begins with a personal or narrative opening that pulls the reader in emotionally.
- Reddit: "Last month I tried [thing] and here's what actually happened" (authenticity)
- TikTok: "Story time: this changed everything for me" (completion bait)
- Facebook: "I never thought I'd share this, but..." (vulnerability drives engagement)
- Instagram: "The moment I realized [insight] was when..." (relatable narrative)

### 4. Contrarian Hook
Takes a position opposite to conventional wisdom.
- Reddit: "Unpopular opinion: [bold claim backed by reasoning]" (drives discussion)
- TikTok: "Stop doing [common thing] — here's why" (pattern interrupt)
- Facebook: "Everyone says [X], but the data shows the opposite" (share-worthy debate)
- Instagram: "[Popular advice] is actually hurting your [goal]" (save-worthy)

### 5. How-To Hook
Promises actionable, practical value.
- Reddit: "[Guide] How to [achieve outcome] in [timeframe] (with examples)" (bookmark-worthy)
- TikTok: "How to [outcome] in 30 seconds" (watch-time promise)
- Facebook: "Here's exactly how I [achieved result] — step by step" (value-driven)
- Instagram: "Save this for later: How to [outcome] ↓" (save trigger)

### 6. List Hook
Uses numbered items to promise organized, scannable value.
- Reddit: "5 things I wish I knew before [activity] — from someone who learned the hard way"
- TikTok: "3 signs you're [identity] and don't know it" (identity + curiosity)
- Facebook: "7 mistakes [audience] makes with [topic] (I made #4 for years)"
- Instagram: "10 [items] that changed my [area] → swipe for the full list"

### 7. Challenge Hook
Dares the reader to try something or tests their knowledge.
- Reddit: "Challenge: Try [action] for 7 days and report back"
- TikTok: "I bet you can't do this" (completion + comment bait)
- Facebook: "Can you spot what's wrong with this [image/scenario]?"
- Instagram: "Try this [technique] and tag me in your results"

### 8. Confession Hook
Shares vulnerability or admits a mistake to build trust and relatability.
- Reddit: "I've been doing [thing] wrong for 5 years. Here's what I learned."
- TikTok: "My biggest mistake was..." (emotional hook)
- Facebook: "I'm embarrassed to admit this, but [relatable confession]"
- Instagram: "Real talk: I failed at [thing] 3 times before it worked"

### 9. Transformation Hook
Shows a before/after or journey of change.
- Reddit: "[Progress] From [starting point] to [result] in [timeframe] — here's how"
- TikTok: "Watch what happens when I [action]" (visual transformation)
- Facebook: "6 months ago vs today — I can't believe the difference"
- Instagram: "Swipe to see the transformation →" (carousel engagement)

### 10. Prediction Hook
Makes a bold forecast about what's coming next.
- Reddit: "Mark my words: [prediction] will happen by [date]. Here's why."
- TikTok: "This is going to be everywhere in 2026" (trend authority)
- Facebook: "I'm calling it now: [prediction]. Save this post."
- Instagram: "The next big trend in [niche] is..." (authority positioning)

### 11. Analogy Hook
Uses a comparison or metaphor to reframe a concept.
- Reddit: "Think of [complex topic] like [simple analogy] — suddenly it makes sense"
- TikTok: "[Topic] is basically [surprising analogy]" (reframe + curiosity)
- Facebook: "Dating is like job hunting. Here's why... " (viral reframe)
- Instagram: "Your [routine] is like a [analogy] — here's how to fix it"

### 12. Warning Hook
Alerts the reader to a risk or danger they may not be aware of.
- Reddit: "PSA: If you're doing [thing], stop immediately. Here's why."
- TikTok: "Warning: Don't [action] until you see this" (urgency + loss aversion)
- Facebook: "If you [common action], you need to read this RIGHT NOW"
- Instagram: "⚠️ Stop [action] — it's actually [consequence]"

## Hook Construction Principles

### The 3-Second Rule
Every hook must communicate its value proposition within 3 seconds of reading/viewing. If the reader can't tell why they should continue, the hook fails.

### Specificity Over Generality
- Weak: "How to be more productive"
- Strong: "How I went from 3 to 8 focused hours per day using one timer technique"

### Emotional Resonance
Hooks that trigger an emotional response (surprise, recognition, curiosity, concern) outperform purely informational hooks by 3-5x.

### The "So What?" Test
After writing every hook, ask: "Would I stop scrolling to read this?" If no, rewrite.

## Hook Testing Framework
- Generate minimum 3-5 variations per content piece
- Vary: trigger type, archetype, length, tone, structure
- Score by: platform fit × trigger clarity × brand alignment × engagement prediction
- A/B test top 2 hooks when possible — each pair must test a distinct hypothesis
- Track: click-through rate, completion rate, save rate, share rate, comment rate
