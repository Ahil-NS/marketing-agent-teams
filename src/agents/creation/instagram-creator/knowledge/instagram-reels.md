# Instagram Reels Strategy

## Trending Audio Identification

### How to Find Trending Audio
- **Reels tab**: Scroll the Reels feed and note recurring audio tracks
- **Audio page**: Tap audio on popular Reels to see usage count and trend direction
- **Trending indicator**: Instagram shows an arrow icon next to trending audio
- **Industry-specific trends**: Track audio trends within your niche, not just overall
- **Cross-platform trends**: TikTok trends often migrate to Reels 1-2 weeks later

### Audio Timing Windows
- Use trending audio within **first 48 hours** of emergence for maximum distribution boost
- Audio trends typically peak at days 3-5, plateau by day 7-10, and decline after day 14
- Original audio CAN perform well but requires strong visual content to compensate
- Music mood categories: upbeat, emotional, cinematic, lo-fi, energetic, ambient

### Audio Best Practices
- Match audio energy to content energy (upbeat audio ≠ calm wellness content)
- Time visual cuts to audio beats for professional feel
- If using voiceover, add background music at 10-20% volume
- When no perfect trending audio exists, use licensed mood-appropriate music

## Hook-First Editing

### The 1-Second Rule
Instagram Reels succeed or fail in the first second. Your hook must:
1. Create a **pattern interrupt** — something unexpected in the viewer's scroll flow
2. Establish a **value promise** — "I'll show you...", "This changed everything...", "Stop doing [X]..."
3. Generate **curiosity gap** — make them need to see what happens next

### Hook Categories (Ranked by Performance)
1. **Shocking statement**: "Nobody told me this about [topic] and I'm upset"
2. **Contrarian take**: "Stop doing [common advice]. Here's why."
3. **Transformation reveal**: "Watch what happens when I try [thing]"
4. **Relatable situation**: "When you [common experience] and realize [insight]..."
5. **Data/number hook**: "I tracked [thing] for 90 days. Here are the numbers."
6. **Tutorial promise**: "How to [achieve result] in [timeframe]"
7. **POV/scenario**: "POV: you just discovered [insight]"

### Visual Hook Techniques
- Start with a close-up face showing emotion
- Begin mid-action (never from a static position)
- Use text overlay that appears instantly (not animated in)
- Open with the most visually interesting moment (then context)
- Use split-screen or before/after as the first frame

## Visual Pattern Interrupts

### Why Pattern Interrupts Matter
The brain naturally stops paying attention after 3-5 seconds of the same stimulus. Pattern interrupts reset attention and boost completion rate.

### Interrupt Types (Rotate These)
| Interrupt Type | Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Camera angle change | Switch from close-up to wide or vice versa | Every 3-5 seconds |
| Text overlay | New text element appears on screen | When introducing a new point |
| B-roll insert | Product shot, environment, relevant footage | To illustrate a concept |
| Speed change | Slow-motion or time-lapse segment | For emphasis or transition |
| Visual effect | Zoom, rotation, split-screen | Sparingly for impact |
| Audio/music shift | Bass drop, beat change, sound effect | To punctuate a key moment |
| Color/filter change | Temperature shift, filter toggle | For before/after or mood shift |

### Interrupt Timing
- 15s Reel: 3-4 interrupts (roughly every 4 seconds)
- 30s Reel: 6-8 interrupts (every 3-4 seconds)
- 60s Reel: 12-15 interrupts (every 4-5 seconds)

## Transition Techniques

### Popular Reel Transitions
1. **Snap transition**: Hand snap → scene change (classic, always works)
2. **Object cover**: Object covers camera → reveals new scene
3. **Matching motion**: Pan right → cut → continue pan in new location
4. **Jump cut**: Quick cuts between angles of the same subject
5. **Swipe**: Hand swipe across camera → new scene
6. **Whip pan**: Fast camera movement → match cut → new angle
7. **Outfit change**: Walk behind furniture → emerge in new outfit

### When NOT to Use Transitions
- Talking-head educational content (transitions distract from message)
- Emotional/vulnerable content (transitions feel artificial)
- Data/statistic reveals (clean cuts work better)

## Text Overlay Timing

### Text Overlay Best Practices
- **Appear early**: Text should be visible within the first 0.5 seconds
- **Duration**: Keep text on screen for at least 2-3 seconds (reading speed)
- **Font size**: Minimum 40pt equivalent — readable on small screens
- **Contrast**: White text on dark background or dark text on light with drop shadow
- **Position**: Center or upper-third (avoid lower area where caption/UI overlaps)
- **Progressive reveal**: Show text one line at a time for guide-style content

### Text Animation Styles
- **Pop-in**: Text appears instantly (fastest to read, best for hooks)
- **Typewriter**: Text types out letter by letter (good for narrative)
- **Slide-in**: Text slides from side (good for lists)
- **Scale-up**: Text zooms from small to large (good for emphasis)

## CTA Placement

### CTA Timing Strategy
- **End-screen CTA** (last 2-3 seconds): "Follow for more", "Save this for later"
- **Mid-Reel CTA** (at value peak): "Comment below if you relate"
- **Caption CTA**: Detailed CTA in the caption (not in the Reel itself)
- **Comment CTA**: Pin a comment with the CTA for additional visibility

### CTA Types by Goal
| Goal | CTA | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| Grow followers | "Follow for more [niche] tips" | End screen |
| Drive saves | "Save this for when you need it" | End screen |
| Drive comments | "Which one resonated most?" | End screen or caption |
| Drive shares | "Share with someone who needs this" | Mid-roll or caption |
| Drive traffic | "Link in bio for the full guide" | End screen + caption |
| Drive DMs | "DM me 'GUIDE' and I'll send it" | Caption |

## Reels vs Stories vs Feed — Algorithm Differences

### Reels Algorithm
- **Discovery-first**: Shown to non-followers more than any other format
- **Completion rate**: THE most important signal — short Reels have advantage
- **Audio usage**: Trending audio gets distribution boost
- **Originality**: Original content > repurposed (watermark detection active)
- **Engagement velocity**: First 30 minutes determine distribution
- **Distribution window**: 24-72 hours for initial distribution, then long-tail

### Stories Algorithm
- **Follower-only**: Only shown to followers (no discovery)
- **Completion rate**: % of frames viewed determines ranking in stories bar
- **Interaction rate**: Replies, sticker interactions boost ranking
- **Recency**: Most recent stories shown first
- **Relationship signal**: Viewers who interact regularly see stories first
- **Distribution window**: 24 hours (ephemeral)

### Feed Algorithm
- **Relationship-based**: Content from accounts users interact with most
- **Interest matching**: Content similar to what users engage with
- **Recency**: Newer posts ranked higher (within interest matching)
- **Content type**: Video slightly prioritized over images in 2026
- **Engagement velocity**: First 30-60 minutes critical for distribution
- **Distribution window**: 24-48 hours for primary distribution

### Format Selection Guide
| Goal | Best Format | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Reach new audiences | Reels | Discovery algorithm, shown to non-followers |
| Deepen existing relationships | Stories | Interactive, personal, follower-only |
| Drive saves/bookmarks | Carousels | Save-worthy educational content |
| Quick engagement | Stories | Polls, quizzes drive easy interaction |
| Brand awareness | Reels + Feed | Combination for reach + retention |
| Conversion/traffic | Stories | Link stickers, swipe-up actions |
