# Instagram Visual Content Strategy

## Art Direction Patterns

### Brand Aesthetic Consistency
Every Instagram account needs a cohesive visual identity. When creating content:

1. **Color palette**: Use 3-5 brand colors consistently across all posts
2. **Typography**: 1-2 fonts maximum — one for headings, one for body text
3. **Photography style**: Consistent editing (warm/cool, saturated/muted, light/dark)
4. **Layout templates**: 2-3 repeatable layouts for different content types
5. **Grid harmony**: Plan how posts look together on the profile grid (9-post grid view)

### Visual Style Categories
- **Clean/Minimal**: White space, geometric layouts, sans-serif fonts, muted colors
- **Bold/Vibrant**: High contrast, saturated colors, large typography, dynamic compositions
- **Warm/Organic**: Earth tones, natural textures, handwritten fonts, soft lighting
- **Dark/Premium**: Dark backgrounds, metallic accents, editorial photography, luxury feel
- **Playful/Fun**: Bright colors, illustrations, hand-drawn elements, casual compositions

## Carousel Slide Design Principles

### Slide Structure
Carousels are Instagram's HIGHEST-performing format for saves. Design each one like a mini-course.

#### Slide 1: The Hook (Cover Slide)
- Bold, clear title text that promises value
- Eye-catching visual that stops the scroll
- Remove all clutter — one message per slide
- Include "Swipe →" indicator or arrow prompt
- Use 4:5 aspect ratio for maximum feed real estate

#### Slides 2-8: The Value
- One key point per slide (never more than one idea)
- Consistent layout template across slides (creates rhythm)
- Progressive reveal: each slide builds on the previous
- Use visual hierarchy: number, heading, supporting text, icon
- Keep text readable at mobile scale (minimum 24pt equivalent)

#### Last Slide: The Payoff
- Summary of key points or final insight
- Clear CTA: "Save for later", "Share with someone who needs this"
- Reminder to follow for more content
- Optional: link in bio mention for deeper content

### Carousel Design Rules
- Maintain consistent margins and padding across all slides
- Use consistent background color or gradient throughout
- Limit to 3 fonts across the carousel (heading, subheading, body)
- Include progress indicators if the narrative requires it
- Swipe narrative: tell a story that makes sense in order AND as individual slides

## Reels Visual Hooks

### First-Frame Design
The first frame determines whether someone watches your Reel. Design for:

1. **Large, bold text**: The hook message in text overlay (readable in 0.5 seconds)
2. **Unexpected visual**: Something that breaks pattern and creates curiosity
3. **Face close-up**: Human faces with expression stop the scroll
4. **Before/After tease**: Show the transformation promise upfront
5. **Movement**: Action in the first frame catches the eye during scroll

### Visual Pattern Interrupts (Every 3-5 Seconds)
- Camera angle change (close-up → wide → over-shoulder)
- Text overlay appearance/change
- B-roll insert (product shot, environment, graphic)
- Speed change (slow-mo → normal → time-lapse)
- Color/filter shift
- Sound effect or music beat sync

## Stories Design Templates

### Interactive Story Sequence (5-7 Frames)
1. **Opener**: Bold statement or question to set context
2. **Poll/Quiz**: Interactive element to drive engagement
3. **Value frame**: Key insight, tip, or information
4. **Visual proof**: Screenshot, photo, or data supporting the point
5. **Question sticker**: "What do you think about [topic]?"
6. **CTA frame**: Link sticker, "DM me", or profile visit prompt
7. **Re-share frame**: "Share this with someone who needs it"

### Story Visual Best Practices
- Full-screen 9:16 format (1080x1920)
- Large text readable without zooming
- High contrast for readability (dark text on light background or vice versa)
- Brand colors as background or accents
- Keep text within the safe zone (avoid edges where UI elements overlap)

## AI Image Generation Prompt Crafting

### Prompt Structure for Instagram Content

When crafting image generation prompts (for Flux, Ideogram, or GPT Image):

```
[Scene description], [Subject/person], [Action/pose], [Lighting],
[Color palette], [Photography style], [Composition], [Mood],
[Technical specifications: aspect ratio, resolution]
```

### Prompt Examples by Content Type

#### Product/Lifestyle Photography
"A person in their late 20s sitting on a sunlit wooden desk, journaling in a leather notebook, warm golden morning light streaming through large windows, earth-tone color palette (cream, tan, forest green), editorial photography style, shallow depth of field, contemplative and peaceful mood, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, high resolution"

#### Flat Lay / Overhead
"Overhead flat lay of wellness essentials on a marble surface: leather journal, herbal tea cup, small succulent plant, candle, and smartphone showing a wellness app dashboard. Soft natural lighting from above, clean minimal aesthetic, neutral colors with one accent color (sage green), 1:1 square aspect ratio"

#### Abstract/Graphic
"Abstract gradient background in warm earth tones (amber, terracotta, cream), subtle organic texture overlays, geometric sans-serif typography placeholder area in center, minimalist aesthetic, Instagram-ready composition, 4:5 portrait aspect ratio"

### Generator-Specific Tips

#### Flux
- Excels at photorealistic images and text rendering
- Include specific lighting and composition details
- Best for: product shots, lifestyle scenes, portraits

#### Ideogram
- Strong text rendering and graphic design
- Great for quote cards, infographics, and branded graphics
- Include font style preferences in prompt
- Best for: carousels, quote graphics, branded content

#### GPT Image
- Versatile across styles (photo, illustration, 3D)
- Good at following complex multi-element compositions
- Include style references (e.g., "in the style of editorial photography")
- Best for: creative concepts, mixed media, conceptual visuals

### Aspect Ratios for Instagram
| Format | Ratio | Dimensions | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Square | 1:1 | 1080x1080 | Standard feed post |
| Portrait | 4:5 | 1080x1350 | Maximum feed real estate |
| Story/Reel | 9:16 | 1080x1920 | Stories and Reels |
| Landscape | 16:9 | 1080x608 | Video thumbnails (not recommended for posts) |
