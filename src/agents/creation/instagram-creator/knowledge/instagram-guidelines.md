# Instagram Content Guidelines

## Algorithm Signals — Key Ranking Factors

Instagram's algorithm in 2026 uses different ranking systems for Feed, Reels, Stories, and Explore. Understanding each is critical.

### Feed & Stories Ranking Signals
1. **Saves**: THE highest-value signal — content saved = content worth seeing again
2. **Shares** (via DM or Stories): Indicates content worth recommending to others
3. **Comments** (especially lengthy ones): Shows content sparked conversation
4. **Likes**: Baseline signal, lower weight than saves/shares
5. **Time spent viewing**: Scrolling past quickly = negative signal
6. **Profile visits after viewing**: Indicates curiosity about the creator

### Explore Page Ranking
- Optimizes for DISCOVERY — shows content to non-followers
- Engagement rate relative to impressions is critical (not raw numbers)
- Saves and shares are weighted 3x more than likes for Explore placement
- Content categorization: algorithm analyzes visual content + captions + hashtags

## Format Best Practices

### Reels
- First frame must stop the scroll — the "hook" concept
- Keep 15-30 seconds for highest completion rate
- Use trending audio within first 48 hours of trend emergence
- On-screen text for sound-off viewing (85% of users browse without sound)
- Pattern interrupts every 3-5 seconds (visual cuts, zoom, text overlay changes)
- Reels distribution is SEPARATE from Feed — different optimization needed
- Original Reels content ranks higher than repurposed TikTok content (watermark detection)

### Carousels
- First slide is the hook — must earn the swipe with value promise
- 5-10 slides for optimal engagement (sweet spot: 7-8 slides)
- Each slide should deliver standalone value (users screenshot individual slides)
- Last slide: CTA, summary, or save-prompt
- Consistent visual style across slides (colors, fonts, layout)
- Carousel saves are THE key algorithm signal — design for save-worthy content
- Swipe completion rate affects distribution — make every slide compelling

### Stories
- Use interactive stickers: polls, quizzes, questions, emoji sliders, countdown
- 3-7 frames per story sequence (optimal engagement)
- Mix media types: photo, video, text, Boomerang
- Include link stickers for conversion (available to all accounts)
- Interactive stickers increase story completion rate by 30-50%
- Stories with polls get 3x more replies than stories without

### Static Posts
- Strong visual with clear focal point — one subject per image
- Text overlays for context when needed (readable at mobile scale)
- Maintain grid aesthetic consistency (color palette, style, spacing)
- Square (1:1) or portrait (4:5) for maximum feed real estate
- High-resolution images: 1080x1080 (square) or 1080x1350 (portrait)

## Hashtag Strategy

### Hashtag Optimization (2026)
- **Optimal count**: 20-25 hashtags per post (research shows this range maximizes reach)
- **Maximum**: 30 per post (Instagram's hard limit)
- **Placement**: Works in both caption and first comment (algorithm treats equally)

### Hashtag Mix Formula
- **3-5 Broad hashtags** (1M+ posts): #wellness #motivation #healthylifestyle
- **10-15 Niche hashtags** (10K-500K posts): #morningroutinetips #mindfulnessjourney
- **2-3 Brand hashtags** (<10K posts): #WellnessApp #WellnessAppTips
- **2-3 Trending/seasonal**: #MentalHealthMonth #SpringWellness2026

### Hashtag Research Rules
- Avoid hashtags with 5M+ posts (too competitive, content gets buried instantly)
- Check hashtag quality: avoid banned/restricted hashtags
- Rotate hashtags every 2-3 posts (prevent hashtag shadow-banning)
- Research competitor hashtags for inspiration, but DON'T copy their set exactly
- Track which hashtag combinations drive the most Explore page appearances

## Caption Optimization

### Caption Structure
1. **Hook line** (first ~125 characters): This appears before "...more" on mobile — MUST be compelling
2. **Value body**: The useful content — tips, insights, story, information
3. **CTA or question**: End with a specific call to action or engagement prompt
4. **Hashtags**: Either in main caption or first comment

### Caption Best Practices
- Start with the most compelling sentence (hook line is everything)
- Provide genuine value in the body — teach, inspire, or entertain
- End with a question or CTA that drives comments or saves
- Use line breaks for readability (no walls of text)
- Include relevant emojis sparingly (2-5 per caption, max)
- Caption length: 150-300 words is optimal for engagement

### Hook Line Examples
- "The one thing nobody tells you about [topic]..."
- "I changed [thing] and this is what happened:"
- "Stop doing [common mistake]. Try this instead:"
- "This simple [technique] changed everything for me."
- "Here's why [common belief] is actually wrong:"
