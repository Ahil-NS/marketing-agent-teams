# Facebook Groups Content Strategy

## Group Selection Criteria

When targeting Facebook Groups for content distribution, evaluate:

### Group Quality Indicators
- **Active membership**: Look for groups with daily posts AND comments (not just member count)
- **Engagement ratio**: Groups with 1,000 members and 50+ daily interactions > groups with 100K members and 10 posts/day
- **Admin responsiveness**: Active admins = healthy community, but also means stricter moderation
- **Topic relevance**: Group must align with campaign themes — never force-fit content
- **Post approval process**: Some groups require post approval — factor in delay to posting schedule

### Group Size Tiers
- **Micro (500-5K)**: Tight-knit community, highest engagement rate, personal connections
- **Mid (5K-50K)**: Good reach with decent engagement, ideal for niche topics
- **Large (50K-500K)**: Wide reach but lower engagement rate, brand awareness plays
- **Mega (500K+)**: Highest reach but posts get buried fast, competitive for attention

## Admin Relationship Building

Before posting promotional content in any Group:

1. **Join genuinely**: Participate as a community member for 2+ weeks before any promotional activity
2. **Contribute value first**: Answer questions, share insights, help other members
3. **Understand group rules**: Read pinned posts, group description, and admin guidelines thoroughly
4. **Connect with admins**: Introduce yourself, ask about content guidelines, offer collaboration
5. **Earn permission**: Many groups allow brand/business posts on specific days (e.g., "Self-Promotion Saturday")

## Value-First Posting Strategy

### The 80/20 Rule for Groups
- 80% of Group posts should be pure value (no product mention, no links)
- 20% can include subtle brand references IF they add genuine value to the conversation

### Value Post Types
- **Expert answers**: Respond to member questions with detailed, helpful answers
- **Polls**: "What's your biggest challenge with [topic]?" — drives engagement AND collects data
- **Guides**: Step-by-step instructions that solve a common problem
- **Discussions**: "What's your experience with [topic]?" — invites sharing and threading
- **Resources**: Curated lists, tools, or approaches (not your own product)

### Group Post Templates
- **Problem-Solution**: "I see [problem] asked about a lot here. Here's what worked: [solution]"
- **Experience Share**: "Just had an experience with [topic] — here's what I learned"
- **Question-First**: "Curious about [topic]. What approach do you take?"
- **Resource Share**: "Found [thing] helpful for [topic]. Has anyone else tried it?"

## Engagement-Driving Questions

### Question Types That Drive Comments
1. **This or That**: "Morning routine or evening wind-down — which impacts your sleep more?"
2. **Ranking**: "Top 3 things that changed your [topic] — go!"
3. **Experience sharing**: "What's one habit you wish you started 5 years ago?"
4. **Debate**: "Unpopular opinion: [provocative but respectful statement]. Thoughts?"
5. **Advice requests**: "Struggling with [thing]. What worked for you?"

### Questions to AVOID
- Yes/no questions (low comment quality)
- Questions that feel like surveys or market research
- Anything requiring personal/sensitive information
- Rhetorical questions that don't invite genuine discussion

## Poll Strategies

### Poll Best Practices
- Limit to 3-5 options (too many reduces participation)
- Include an "Other (comment below)" option to drive comments
- Time-limited polls for urgency (24-48 hours)
- Follow up on poll results with a summary post — drives secondary engagement

### Poll Types
- **Preference polls**: "Which do you prefer: A, B, or C?"
- **Awareness polls**: "Have you tried [topic]? Yes / No / What's that?"
- **Scheduling polls**: "What time works best for a live Q&A? Morning / Afternoon / Evening"
- **Feedback polls**: "What topic should we cover next?"

## Live Video Considerations

### When to Go Live in Groups
- Q&A sessions on hot topics within the group
- Behind-the-scenes looks that provide unique value
- Expert interviews relevant to group interests
- Event recaps or real-time reactions

### Live Video Best Practices
- Announce 24 hours in advance with a preview post
- Keep sessions 15-30 minutes for Groups (attention span shorter than Page audiences)
- Engage with comments in real-time — this is the primary value driver
- Save and pin the replay for members who missed it
- Follow up with a summary post highlighting key takeaways

## Anti-Spam Compliance

### Red Flags That Get Posts Removed
- Posting the same content across multiple groups simultaneously
- Including links in every post (even if relevant)
- New member immediately posting promotional content
- Using group-inappropriate sales language
- Ignoring group-specific posting rules or schedules

### Safe Practices
- Space Group posts by 24-48 hours minimum
- Customize content for each group's specific culture and language
- Use different angles/hooks for the same topic across different groups
- Build a posting history of value posts before any promotional posts
- Always disclose affiliations when relevant
