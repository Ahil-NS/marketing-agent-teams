# Facebook Content Guidelines

## Algorithm Signals — Meaningful Interactions Priority

Facebook's algorithm in 2026 prioritizes "meaningful interactions" above all else. The ranking order:

1. **Comments** (especially long comments with depth > 2 replies) — HIGHEST signal
2. **Shares** to personal feeds, Groups, and Messenger — drives viral distribution
3. **Reactions** (Love, Haha, Wow > basic Like) — indicate emotional engagement
4. **Video watch time** — critical for video content ranking
5. **Click-throughs** — matter less than on-platform engagement
6. **Saves** — growing signal in 2026, indicates high-value content

### Comment Depth Multiplier
- A post with 10 comments of 2+ replies each ranks FAR higher than one with 50 simple comments
- Threading conversations is the #1 organic reach strategy
- Ask specific questions that invite detailed responses, not simple yes/no

### Share Mechanics
- Shares to personal feed: 2x weight vs reactions
- Shares to Groups: 3x weight (content enters a new distribution pool)
- Shares via Messenger: Highest signal but hardest to earn
- Content must be share-worthy: useful, surprising, or emotionally resonant

## Content Types & Performance Rankings

1. **Native video** (uploaded directly, not linked): Highest organic reach
2. **Facebook Reels**: Growing distribution channel, separate algorithm from Feed
3. **Image posts**: Strong organic reach, especially multi-image carousels
4. **Text posts** (opinion, question, hot take): Drive comments, good organic reach
5. **Carousel posts**: Step-by-step guides, product features, educational content
6. **Link posts**: LOWEST organic reach — algorithm deprioritizes off-platform links

## Community Engagement Strategy

- Groups drive highest organic reach on Facebook in 2026
- Pages should focus on shareable, comment-worthy content
- Events create urgency and notification advantages
- Polls drive easy engagement AND collect audience data
- Live Video gets notification priority and real-time engagement

## Copy Best Practices

- Lead with value or provocative statement that stops the scroll
- Keep primary text under 125 characters for mobile (before "See more" truncation)
- Use emojis sparingly for emphasis — max 2-3 per post
- Include clear CTA: ask a question, invite sharing, or request opinions
- Use line breaks for readability on mobile
- Avoid ALL engagement bait patterns (tag someone, like if you agree, share to win)

## Engagement Bait Penalties

Facebook ACTIVELY PENALIZES these patterns — NEVER use them:
- "Tag a friend who..." — demoted in Feed ranking
- "Like if you agree, react if you don't" — classified as engagement bait
- "Share to win" or "Comment to enter" — requires official contest rules
- Click-bait headlines that misrepresent content
- Sensationalized or misleading thumbnails for video content

## Post Formatting

- **Character limits**: 63,206 characters max, but keep under 500 for best engagement
- **Mobile-first**: 80%+ users are on mobile — test formatting on mobile view
- **Link previews**: When using links, customize the preview image and title
- **Video specs**: 16:9 or 1:1 for Feed, 9:16 for Reels/Stories, auto-captions recommended
- **Image specs**: 1200x630 for links, 1080x1080 for square posts, high-res PNG/JPG
