# Facebook Content Guidelines

## Algorithm Signals
- Comments (especially long comments) are highest signal
- Shares to personal feeds and groups boost reach
- Reactions indicate engagement but less than comments
- Video watch time is critical for video content
- Click-throughs matter less than on-platform engagement

## Content Types
- **Text posts**: Opinion, question, hot take — drive comments
- **Image posts**: Infographic, quote card, behind-the-scenes
- **Video posts**: Tutorial, testimonial, live replay
- **Carousel posts**: Step-by-step guides, product features
- **Link posts**: Blog articles, resources (lower organic reach)

## Community Engagement
- Groups drive highest organic reach on Facebook
- Pages should focus on shareable, comment-worthy content
- Events create urgency and notification advantages
- Polls drive easy engagement and data collection

## Copy Best Practices
- Lead with value or provocative statement
- Keep primary text under 125 characters for mobile
- Use emojis sparingly for emphasis
- Include clear CTA (ask a question, invite sharing)
