# Facebook Algorithm Optimization

## Meaningful Interaction Framework

Facebook's algorithm in 2026 is built around the concept of "meaningful interactions" — content
that drives real conversations, not passive consumption. Understanding this framework is critical
for organic reach.

### Interaction Hierarchy (Weight Order)
1. **Comments with replies** (threaded conversations): The #1 ranking signal
2. **Shares to personal feed**: Content endorsed by a user to their own network
3. **Shares to Groups**: Cross-community distribution, creates new engagement pools
4. **Shares via Messenger**: Private sharing, highest trust signal
5. **Love/Wow/Haha reactions**: Emotional engagement, weighted higher than basic Like
6. **Basic Like**: Lowest engagement signal, still counts but minimally
7. **Clicks without engagement**: Almost zero ranking benefit

### Comment Threading — The Organic Reach Multiplier
- A post with 5 comments that each generate 3+ replies outranks a post with 50 standalone comments
- The algorithm rewards posts that spark CONVERSATIONS, not just reactions
- Strategy: Ask specific, open-ended questions that invite detailed responses
- Reply to EVERY comment in the first 2 hours — this trains the algorithm that your post drives interaction

## Share Mechanics

### Types of Shares & Their Impact
| Share Type | Algorithm Weight | Reach Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Share to personal Timeline | 2x vs Like | Introduces to entire friend network |
| Share to Group | 3x vs Like | Enters new distribution pool entirely |
| Share via Messenger | 4x vs Like | Highest trust, hardest to earn |
| Copy link (external) | 1x baseline | Minimal algorithm impact |

### Creating Share-Worthy Content
Content gets shared when it makes the sharer look good. People share content that:
- **Makes them look informed**: Data, insights, surprising facts
- **Makes them look caring**: Causes, community support, helpful resources
- **Makes them look funny**: Humor that's relatable and safe to share
- **Solves a problem**: Practical tips, guides, life hacks
- **Validates their identity**: Content that says "this is who I am"

## Video Priority & Distribution

### Video Algorithm Signals
- **Watch time**: Most critical — videos watched >50% get massive distribution
- **Completion rate**: Videos watched to the end get ranked highest
- **Replays**: Content that gets rewatched signals high value
- **Audio on vs off**: Audio-on viewing counts more than auto-play muted viewing
- **Native upload**: Direct uploads rank FAR higher than YouTube/external links
- **Live video**: Gets notification priority and real-time engagement boost

### Video Best Practices for Algorithm
- Hook within first 3 seconds to prevent scroll-past
- Optimal length: 1-3 minutes for Feed, 15-60 seconds for Reels
- Add captions/subtitles: 85% of Facebook video is watched without sound
- Upload natively — NEVER post YouTube links for organic content
- Square (1:1) or portrait (9:16) for mobile-first viewing

## Facebook Reels Distribution

### Reels Algorithm (Separate from Feed)
Facebook Reels has its own distribution algorithm, separate from the main Feed:
- **Discovery-focused**: Reels are shown to non-followers more than Feed posts
- **Completion rate is king**: Short Reels (15-30s) with high completion rate win
- **Audio matters**: Trending audio gets distribution boost
- **Originality bonus**: Original content ranks higher than repurposed content
- **Cross-posting penalty**: Content detected as identical to Instagram Reels may get deprioritized

### Reels vs Feed Algorithm Differences
| Signal | Feed | Reels |
|---|---|---|
| Network importance | HIGH (friends/following) | LOW (discovery-first) |
| Content relevance | HIGH (topic matching) | MEDIUM (broader interests) |
| Completion rate | MEDIUM | HIGHEST |
| Engagement velocity | HIGH (first 2 hrs) | MEDIUM (24-48 hr window) |
| Audio/music use | LOW impact | HIGH impact |

## Engagement Bait Detection & Penalties

### Patterns That Trigger Penalties
Facebook uses ML classifiers to detect engagement bait. These patterns result in Feed ranking demotion:

1. **Vote baiting**: "Like for X, Heart for Y" or any reaction-based voting
2. **Tag baiting**: "Tag a friend who..." or "Tag someone who needs this"
3. **Share baiting**: "Share this to your wall" or "Share if you agree"
4. **Comment baiting**: "Comment YES if you agree" or "Type 'amen'"
5. **React baiting**: "React with 😡 if this makes you angry"

### Penalty Severity
- First offense: 20-30% reach reduction on that post
- Repeated offenses: Page-level demotion affecting ALL posts
- Severe cases: Content warning labels or feature restrictions

### Safe Alternatives
Instead of engagement bait, use these patterns:
- "What's your experience with [topic]?" (genuine question)
- "I'd love to hear your thoughts on [topic]" (invitation to discuss)
- "Has anyone else noticed [observation]?" (conversation starter)
- Share genuinely useful content that people WANT to share organically

## Organic Reach Factors

### Posting Timing
- Post when YOUR audience is online (check Page Insights, not generic "best times")
- Engagement in the first 30-60 minutes determines algorithmic distribution
- Be online to respond to comments immediately after posting

### Posting Frequency
- Pages: 1-2 posts per day maximum (quality over quantity)
- Groups: 1-3 posts per week (too frequent = spam perception)
- Reels: Daily posting is acceptable and encouraged
- Stories: 3-7 per day is optimal (ephemeral, doesn't clog Feed)

### Content Format Optimization
- Mix content formats: algorithm rewards variety (text, image, video, link)
- Don't post the same format 3+ times in a row
- Video content should make up 30-50% of posting mix
- Carousel/multi-image posts drive saves and longer viewing time:

### Negative Signals to Avoid
- Posting links to low-quality websites
- Excessive posting (>3 posts/day from a Page)
- Clickbait headlines that don't match content
- Edited thumbnails that are misleading
- Content flagged or reported by users
- Duplicate content across multiple Pages/Groups
