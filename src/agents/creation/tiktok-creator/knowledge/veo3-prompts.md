# Veo 3 Video Generation Prompt Patterns

Veo 3 is Google's AI video generation model. These prompt patterns produce
high-quality, brand-aligned video content for TikTok.

## Prompt Structure Template

A complete Veo 3 prompt should follow this structure:

```
[Scene description]. [Subject/actor description and actions].
[Visual style and aesthetic]. [Camera movements and transitions].
[Lighting and color]. [Duration specification].
[Audio/music direction if applicable].
```

## Scene Description Structure

### Setting
- "A bright, modern kitchen with natural morning light streaming through windows"
- "An urban rooftop at golden hour with city skyline in the background"
- "A cozy home office with warm lighting and minimal decor"
- "A gym environment with dynamic overhead lighting"

### Composition
- "Close-up shot of hands [performing action]"
- "Medium shot of a person sitting at a desk, facing camera"
- "Wide establishing shot that slowly tracks in to medium close-up"
- "Split-screen showing before and after simultaneously"

## Visual Style Directives

### Aesthetic Options
| Style | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Cinematic | Shallow depth of field, warm color grading, cinema aspect | Brand stories, premium products |
| Lo-fi | Slightly grainy, muted colors, handheld feel | Authenticity, relatable content |
| Clean | Bright, high-contrast, crisp edges, white backgrounds | Tutorials, product demos |
| Vibrant | Saturated colors, high energy, dynamic lighting | Entertainment, trend content |
| Raw | Unprocessed look, natural lighting, no filters | Behind-the-scenes, day-in-life |
| Editorial | High-fashion inspired, dramatic lighting, strong composition | Lifestyle, aspirational content |

### Color Direction
- "Warm color palette with amber tones" — inviting, comfortable
- "Cool blue-teal color grading" — professional, tech-focused
- "High saturation with punchy greens and oranges" — energetic, youthful
- "Desaturated with selective color pop" — dramatic, attention-grabbing
- "Natural color with slightly lifted blacks" — authentic, approachable

## Motion and Transition Vocabulary

### Camera Movements
- **Push in / Track in**: Slowly moving toward subject — builds intensity
- **Pull back / Track out**: Moving away from subject — reveals context
- **Pan**: Horizontal sweep — shows environment or connects elements
- **Tilt up/down**: Vertical movement — reveals from bottom to top or vice versa
- **Handheld**: Slight natural shake — adds authenticity and energy
- **Drone-style overhead**: Bird's eye view — establishing or transition shot
- **Whip pan**: Fast horizontal sweep — energetic transition between scenes

### Transition Types
- **Match cut**: Similar shapes/movements across scenes — seamless flow
- **Jump cut**: Quick skip in time — pacing and energy
- **Dissolve/crossfade**: Smooth blend between scenes — passage of time
- **Swipe transition**: Screen wipe matching a physical movement — TikTok native feel
- **Zoom transition**: Zoom into detail then zoom out to new scene

## Duration Specifications

- **15s**: "15-second continuous shot" or "15 seconds, 3 cuts at 5s intervals"
- **30s**: "30-second video with 5-6 scene changes" (most common TikTok format)
- **60s**: "60-second video, 8-10 scene transitions, story arc structure"

### Pacing
- "Quick cuts every 2-3 seconds" — high energy, younger audience
- "Smooth, held shots of 4-5 seconds each" — calming, informational
- "Accelerating pace — slow start, increasingly fast cuts" — building excitement

## Audio and Music Cues

### Music Direction (when not using trending sounds)
- "Upbeat lo-fi hip hop beat, 90 BPM" — casual, relatable
- "Energetic electronic pop, 120+ BPM" — exciting, dynamic
- "Acoustic guitar, gentle and warm" — storytelling, emotional
- "Dramatic orchestral swell building to climax" — reveal moments
- "Ambient, minimal background tone" — voiceover-focused content

### Sound Effects
- "Satisfying click/snap sound on text appearance"
- "Whoosh transition sounds between scenes"
- "Pop sound effect emphasizing key text revelations"
- "Natural ambient sounds (coffee pour, keyboard typing)" — ASMR-adjacent

## Brand-Aligned Visual Language

### Consistency Elements to Specify
1. **Color palette**: Match brand colors in backgrounds, text, or accents
2. **Font style**: Specify on-screen text font characteristics (bold, sans-serif, etc.)
3. **Subject appearance**: Relatable to target demographic
4. **Environment**: Consistent with brand's lifestyle positioning
5. **Energy level**: Match brand voice (calm authority vs energetic enthusiasm)

### Example Complete Prompts

**Tutorial style:**
"A close-up overhead shot of hands preparing a morning smoothie in a bright, clean white kitchen. Natural morning sunlight streams from the left. Quick cuts between adding each ingredient. Clean aesthetic with vibrant fruit colors against white countertop. Camera slowly pushes in from overhead to 45-degree angle. 30 seconds, 6 cuts. Upbeat lo-fi background music."

**Storytelling style:**
"A young professional sits at a modern desk, looking at camera. Medium close-up, shallow depth of field. Warm tungsten lighting, cozy home office setting. Handheld camera feel with subtle movement. Person speaks directly to camera with expressive hand gestures. 60 seconds, occasional cut to relevant B-roll inserts. Natural room tone audio."

**Product reveal:**
"Dramatic close-up of product on dark background, single spotlight from above. Camera slowly circles the product. Cinematic style, high contrast, dark with selective warm highlights. Smooth dolly movement, 180-degree orbit. At 5 seconds, quick zoom into product detail. 15 seconds. Dramatic orchestral note building to satisfying reveal sound."
