# TikTok SEO Deep Dive

TikTok has evolved from an entertainment platform to a search engine. Over 40% of Gen Z
users prefer TikTok over Google for discovery. SEO optimization is critical for
long-term content visibility beyond the initial For You Page push.

## Keyword Research Methodology

### Step 1: Identify Core Keywords
- Start with the campaign plan's content themes
- Extract key terms from the target audience description
- List problems the brand solves ("how to...", "best way to...", "why does...")

### Step 2: Expand with TikTok Search Suggest
- Type core keywords in TikTok search bar — note auto-complete suggestions
- These suggestions are based on REAL user search queries
- Each suggestion is a potential content keyword target
- Note "Others searched for" results at bottom of search pages

### Step 3: Validate with Hashtag Volume
- Check hashtag view counts for each keyword phrase
- Sweet spot: 10M-500M views (enough volume, not too competitive)
- Ultra-trending (1B+ views) — your content may get buried
- Very niche (under 1M views) — limited discovery potential

### Step 4: Map Keywords to Content
- Assign 1-2 primary keywords per content piece
- Each primary keyword should appear in ALL four SEO layers
- Secondary keywords (2-3) should appear in at least 2 layers

## Caption Keyword Placement

### Priority Positions
1. **First 3 words**: Highest weight in TikTok's search algorithm
2. **First line** (before "...more"): Visible without expansion — must contain primary keyword
3. **Natural integration**: Keywords must read naturally — keyword stuffing is penalized

### Caption Formulas That Work
- "[Keyword] hack you NEED to try" — search + curiosity
- "How I [keyword] in [timeframe]" — search + social proof
- "POV: you finally found the right [keyword]" — search + trend format
- "[Number] [keyword] tips nobody talks about" — search + list format
- "Replying to @user — here's my [keyword] routine" — reply + search + authenticity

### Caption Length for SEO
- Short (40-80 chars): Higher engagement rate, lower search surface
- Medium (80-150 chars): Best balance of engagement + SEO
- Long (150-300 chars): Maximum SEO surface, lower engagement rate
- Recommendation: 80-150 characters with 1-2 keyword phrases

## Hashtag Hierarchy

### Tier 1: Trending Hashtags (1-2)
- **Volume**: 100M+ views
- **Purpose**: Broad reach, FYP candidacy
- **Lifespan**: 3-7 days (must be CURRENTLY trending)
- **Examples**: #lifehack, #motivation, #fyp (use sparingly — very competitive)
- **Selection**: Choose trending hashtags that authentically relate to content

### Tier 2: Niche Hashtags (2-3)
- **Volume**: 10M-100M views
- **Purpose**: Targeted audience reach, community discovery
- **Lifespan**: Weeks to months (more stable)
- **Examples**: #morningroutine, #wellnesstips, #productivityhacks
- **Selection**: Match the specific content topic and target audience interest

### Tier 3: Brand Hashtags (1)
- **Volume**: Varies (you build this)
- **Purpose**: Community building, content tracking, UGC aggregation
- **Lifespan**: Permanent (you own this)
- **Examples**: #YourBrandChallenge, #YourBrandTips
- **Selection**: Short, memorable, brandable, not already used by others

### Hashtag Anti-Patterns (Avoid)
- #fyp, #foryou, #foryoupage as sole strategy — they're noise, not signal
- Using 15+ hashtags — dilutes algorithm signals
- Hashtags that don't match content — damages content quality score
- Using banned or suppressed hashtags (TikTok silently suppresses some)
- Same hashtag set on every post — signals automation

## Search Ranking Signals

### Content-Based Signals
1. Keyword match between search query and caption/audio/OCR text
2. Content relevance score (based on visual and audio analysis)
3. Engagement rate on the content (higher engagement = higher ranking)
4. Recency (newer content ranks higher for most queries)

### Account-Based Signals
1. Niche consistency — accounts that consistently post about one topic rank higher
2. Follower engagement rate — accounts with engaged followers get preference
3. Content velocity — regular posting frequency is rewarded
4. Authority indicators — verified badge, high engagement history

### User-Based Signals
1. Previously watched similar content
2. Followed accounts in the niche
3. Saved or shared related content
4. Geographic and demographic relevance

## Explore Page Optimization

### How to Get Featured on Explore
- Content must be in a trending or high-growth topic
- High completion rate signals (>80% average watch time)
- Strong initial engagement in first 30 minutes of posting
- Use trending sounds within first 48 hours of the sound trending
- Content that generates saves (not just likes) has higher Explore candidacy

### Explore Page Content Characteristics
- Clean, high-quality visuals (1080p minimum)
- Clear on-screen text that's readable on mobile
- Content that works without sound (subtitles/text overlays)
- Loop-friendly structure (end connects to beginning)
- Content that generates shares and comments (not just views)

## Search vs. FYP Optimization Tradeoffs

| Factor | FYP Optimization | Search Optimization |
|---|---|---|
| Content freshness | Critical — works for 24-72h | Less important — evergreen content ranks longer |
| Trending sounds | Major boost | Minor factor |
| Keywords | Nice to have | Essential across all 4 layers |
| Hashtags | 3-5 max | 4-6 targeted keywords |
| Caption length | Short (40-80 chars) | Medium-long (80-150 chars) |
| Content type | Entertaining, novel | Informational, searchable |

**Recommendation**: Target both FYP and Search — use trending sounds + keyword-rich captions + niche hashtags for maximum distribution across both discovery channels.
