# Veo 3 Video Generation Prompt Patterns for TikTok

## Veo 3 Prompt Structure

Every Veo 3 video prompt should follow this structured format:

```
[Scene description]. [Subject/actor description]. [Visual style]. [Camera movement]. [Transitions]. [Duration]. [Audio/music cues]. [Brand alignment].
```

## Scene Description Structure

### Physical Setting
- **Location:** Interior/exterior, specific environment (bedroom, street, kitchen, studio)
- **Lighting:** Natural light, golden hour, studio lighting, neon, dramatic shadows
- **Time of day:** Morning warmth, midday brightness, evening golden hour, night
- **Atmosphere:** Clean and modern, cozy and warm, urban and energetic, minimal

### Example Scene Descriptions:
> A bright, modern bedroom with natural morning sunlight streaming through sheer curtains. Warm amber color grading, soft diffused light creating gentle shadows on a wooden desk.

> An outdoor tree-lined sidewalk during golden hour. Warm, natural lighting with long shadows. Slightly overcast sky providing soft, even illumination.

## Camera Movement Vocabulary

| Movement | Description | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Static** | Camera stays fixed | Talking head, product showcase |
| **Pan** (left/right) | Camera rotates horizontally on axis | Revealing a scene, following action |
| **Tilt** (up/down) | Camera rotates vertically on axis | Dramatic reveal, showing scale |
| **Tracking** | Camera moves with the subject | Walking shots, following movement |
| **Dolly** (in/out) | Camera moves toward/away from subject | Dramatic emphasis, reveal |
| **Drone/aerial** | High-angle overhead movement | Establishing shot, context |
| **Handheld** | Slight natural shake | Authentic feel, vlog style |
| **Zoom** (in/out) | Lens zoom without camera movement | Emphasis, dramatic moment |
| **Push-in** | Slow forward camera movement | Building tension, focus |
| **Pull-back** | Camera moves backward from subject | Reveal context, ending shot |
| **Orbit** | Camera circles around subject | Product showcase, dramatic |
| **Crane** | Vertical camera movement on boom | Dramatic reveal, transition |

### Movement Pacing:
- **Slow** — Contemplative, artistic, luxury feel
- **Medium** — Natural, comfortable, conversational
- **Fast** — Energetic, urgent, exciting
- **Whip** — Ultra-fast snap between positions (pattern interrupt)

## Transition Types

| Transition | Description | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut** | Instant switch between scenes | Fast-paced content, standard |
| **Jump cut** | Cut within same frame (skip time) | TikTok-native, authentic feel |
| **Smooth zoom** | Zoom into one scene, zoom out of next | Seamless scene change |
| **Match cut** | Object/shape matches between scenes | Creative storytelling |
| **Swipe** | Content slides in direction | List reveals, before/after |
| **Morph** | One image transforms into another | Transformations, comparisons |
| **Flash** | Quick white/black frame | Energy, emphasis |
| **Split screen** | Two scenes side by side | Before/after, comparison |
| **Text pop-in** | Text elements animate onto screen | Key points, data reveals |

## Duration Control

| TikTok Format | Duration | Prompt Specification |
|---|---|---|
| Quick tip | 15 seconds | "15 seconds, 3 scene transitions" |
| Standard | 30 seconds | "30 seconds, 5-6 scene transitions" |
| Deep dive | 60 seconds | "60 seconds, 8-10 scene transitions" |

**Rule of thumb:** 1 scene transition every 3-5 seconds keeps engagement high.

## Music/Audio Cue Integration

### Music Mood Categories:
- **Lo-fi hip hop** — Chill, morning routines, study, wellness
- **Upbeat electronic** — Energy, motivation, fitness, productivity
- **Acoustic/indie** — Authentic, heartfelt, storytelling
- **Cinematic orchestral** — Dramatic reveals, transformation, epic content
- **Trending audio** — Reference specific trending sounds by name when available

### Audio Cue Prompt Format:
```
[Music type] at [volume level]. [Sound effects if any]. [Narration style if applicable].
```

**Example:**
> Lo-fi hip hop background music at low volume. Subtle page-turning sound effect at 5s. Natural ambient sounds (birdsong) during outdoor walking scene.

## Text Overlay Timing

- **First 2 seconds:** Hook text — large, bold, center-screen
- **Every 3-5 seconds:** New text element to maintain attention
- **Final 3 seconds:** CTA text — clear, actionable

### Text Style for TikTok:
- High contrast (white on dark, or dark on light with shadow)
- Large enough to read on mobile (minimum 24pt equivalent)
- Short phrases (3-7 words per overlay)
- Animated entrance (pop-in, slide-in, typewriter)

## Brand-Aligned Visual Styles

| Style | Visual Profile | Brand Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Cinematic** | Warm grading, shallow DOF, 24fps feel | Premium, aspirational |
| **Lo-fi** | Slightly desaturated, natural grain, handheld | Authentic, relatable, wellness |
| **Clean** | High contrast, crisp, minimal distractions | Professional, tech, data |
| **Vibrant** | Saturated colors, dynamic lighting, bold | Youthful, energetic, fun |
| **Raw** | Minimal grading, natural textures, unpolished | Authentic, real talk, transparency |
| **Editorial** | Magazine-quality, styled, composed | Fashion, lifestyle, luxury |

## Complete Veo 3 Prompt Example

> A young professional in their late 20s sits on a bed in a bright, modern bedroom with natural morning sunlight streaming through sheer curtains. Warm amber color grading, shallow depth of field. Camera starts in close-up on face with genuine expression, then cuts to overhead shot of hands writing in a leather journal on a wooden desk. Smooth transition to outdoor walking shot on a tree-lined street, golden hour lighting, handheld camera feel with slight natural movement. Cut to kitchen scene, clean countertop, pouring water into a glass with coffee maker visible in background. Match cut to side-by-side infographic showing before/after metrics with text pop-in animation. End on medium shot of person smiling genuinely at camera. 30 seconds, 5 scene transitions. Lo-fi hip hop background music at low volume, subtle ambient sounds during outdoor scene. Brand colors: warm earth tones (amber, cream, sage green) throughout.
