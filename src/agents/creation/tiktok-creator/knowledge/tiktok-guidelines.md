# TikTok Content Guidelines

## Hook Patterns (First 2 Seconds)
- "POV: you just discovered..."
- "Wait for it..." with visual preview
- Bold text overlay with surprising statement
- Before/after reveal setup
- "I tested X for 30 days — here's what happened"
- Direct question to viewer

## Content Structures
- **Tutorial**: Hook → Steps → Result → CTA
- **Storytime**: Hook → Setup → Conflict → Resolution
- **List format**: "3 things you didn't know about..."
- **Reaction**: React to trend/video with brand angle
- **Day-in-the-life**: Relatable routine content

## Algorithm Optimization
- Completion rate is the #1 metric — keep viewers watching
- Pattern interrupts every 3-5 seconds maintain attention
- Trending sounds give algorithmic boost (use within first 48h of trending)
- Post during peak hours: 7-9am, 12-3pm, 7-11pm (audience timezone)
- Consistent posting schedule (1-3x daily for growth)

## Caption Strategy
- Keep captions short (40-80 characters)
- Use 3-5 hashtags: 1 trending + 2 niche + 1-2 brand
- Include CTA in caption when not in video
- Use line breaks for readability
