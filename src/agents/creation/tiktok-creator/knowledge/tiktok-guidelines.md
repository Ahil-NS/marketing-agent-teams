# TikTok Content Guidelines

## Hook Patterns (First 2 Seconds)
The hook is the single most critical element. It determines whether users stop scrolling.

### Visual Hooks
- Bold text overlay with surprising statement or statistic
- Before/after reveal setup (show the "after" first)
- Extreme close-up that pulls back to reveal context
- Pattern interrupt: unexpected visual that breaks the scroll pattern

### Verbal Hooks
- "POV: you just discovered..."
- "Wait for it..." with visual preview
- "I tested X for 30 days — here's what happened"
- Direct question: "Did you know that...?"
- "Stop scrolling if you..."
- Controversial opener: "Unpopular opinion: ..."
- "3 things nobody tells you about..."

### Hook Timing Rules
- Hook must hit within 0.5 seconds — not 2 full seconds
- The "2-second rule" means the hook must COMPLETE by 2 seconds
- Front-load the most compelling visual or statement
- If using text, it must appear frame 1 — no fade-in

## Content Structures

### Tutorial Format (Best for educational content)
Hook → Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → Result → CTA
- Pattern interrupt between each step (zoom, cut, text change)
- Keep steps to 3-5 maximum
- Show the final result in the first 2 seconds, then "here's how"

### Storytime Format (Best for brand narratives)
Hook → Setup → Conflict → Resolution → Lesson
- Use first-person narration
- Include emotional beats
- End with a question to viewers

### List Format (Best for engagement)
"3 things you didn't know about..."
- Number each item with on-screen text
- Most surprising item LAST (keeps viewers watching)
- Use transition effects between items

### Reaction Format (Best for trend riding)
React to trend/video/comment with brand angle
- Show the source content briefly
- Add your unique perspective
- Design for duet/stitch compatibility

### Day-in-the-Life Format (Best for authenticity)
Relatable routine content showing real process
- Behind-the-scenes authenticity
- Imperfect is better than polished
- Include relatable moments (coffee, struggles, wins)

## 4-Layer SEO Breakdown

### Layer 1: Caption SEO
- Front-load keywords in first 40 characters
- Use conversational keyword phrases matching search intent
- Include question keywords ("how to X", "why does Y")
- People search TikTok like Google — optimize for search queries
- Caption length: 40-80 characters for engagement, up to 150 for SEO

### Layer 2: OCR Text Optimization
- TikTok's computer vision reads on-screen text and indexes it for search
- Place keyword-rich text in the first 3 seconds
- Use clear, readable fonts (no script fonts)
- Text must contrast with background (white on dark, black on light)
- Include the primary keyword as on-screen text at least once

### Layer 3: Audio Keyword Density
- TikTok auto-transcribes audio for search indexing
- Say your target keyword 2-3 times naturally in the script
- Speak clearly near keyword phrases (mumbling = missed indexing)
- Audio keywords compound with caption and OCR keywords
- If using trending sound, overlay voiceover with keywords

### Layer 4: Hashtag SEO
- Tier 1 (trending): 1-2 hashtags with 100M+ views — broad reach
- Tier 2 (niche): 2-3 hashtags with 10M-100M views — targeted audience
- Tier 3 (brand): 1 branded hashtag — tracking and community building
- Total: 4-6 hashtags per post
- Place most important hashtags first in caption
- Trending hashtags rotate every 48-72 hours — always use current ones

## For You Page (FYP) Algorithm Signals

### Positive Signals (increase distribution)
1. **Completion rate**: #1 signal — videos watched to the end get wider distribution
2. **Rewatch rate**: Videos that loop or get rewatched signal high quality
3. **Share rate**: Videos shared via DM or external get algorithmic boost
4. **Comment engagement**: Videos with conversations in comments rank higher
5. **Profile visits**: If viewers visit your profile after watching, strong positive signal
6. **Sound usage timing**: Using sounds within 48h of trending gives early-mover boost

### Negative Signals (suppress distribution)
1. **Skip rate**: Users scrolling past quickly = negative signal
2. **Not interested** taps: Strong suppression signal
3. **Excessive hashtags**: 10+ hashtags can appear spammy
4. **Low resolution**: Under 720p gets suppressed
5. **Watermarks**: Other platform watermarks (especially Instagram) get penalized
6. **Duplicate content**: Exact reposts get suppressed after initial views

## Completion Rate Optimization Techniques

### Short Content (15s)
- One clear idea, one hook, one payoff
- No wasted seconds — every moment must earn attention
- Loop-friendly: end connects to beginning visually

### Medium Content (30s)
- Pattern interrupt every 5-7 seconds
- Build curiosity throughout — reveal or payoff at end
- Use on-screen text to guide viewers through sections

### Long Content (60s)
- Pattern interrupt every 3-5 seconds (more frequent for longer content)
- Mid-video hook at 30s mark to retain viewers
- Preview the payoff early but deliver it at the END
- Use numbering or progress indicators ("step 2 of 5")

### Pattern Interrupt Techniques
- Camera angle change (close-up ↔ wide)
- Text overlay pop-in
- Sound effect or music change
- Scene or background transition
- Zoom or pan movement
- Visual effect (filter, sticker, transition)
- B-roll cut to supporting visual

## Algorithm-Optimized Posting Times
- Morning: 7-9 AM (audience timezone) — commute scrolling
- Midday: 12-3 PM — lunch break content consumption
- Evening: 7-11 PM — prime time, highest overall engagement
- Post during YOUR audience's timezone, not yours
- Consistent scheduling trains the algorithm to distribute your content
- Growth phase: 1-3 posts per day for fastest audience building
